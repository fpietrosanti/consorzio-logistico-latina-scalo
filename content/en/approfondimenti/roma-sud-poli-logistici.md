The southern quadrant of Rome, between the SS148 Pontina state road, Via Ardeatina, Via Laurentina and the coast as far as Fiumicino, hosts some of the main logistics areas in Lazio. The Consorzio Industriale del Lazio counts two industrial areas there, Santa Palomba with 483 hectares between Rome and Pomezia and Castel Romano with 266 hectares at km 23 of the Pontina, to which are added the logistics areas of Pomezia, the Santa Palomba intermodal terminal, the largest in Lazio by intermodal transport units, and the Fiumicino airport system. New logistics developments in Pomezia and Santa Palomba were announced in 2026. Further south along the same Pontina, the [province of Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/provincia-di-latina" >}}) offers industrial areas with characteristics of their own.

## Santa Palomba

The industrial area of [Santa Palomba]({{< relref "/logistica/santa-palomba" >}}), a district of Pomezia, is the largest in the former Rome-Latina consortium area: 483 hectares, of which 318 zoned for industrial use, according to the Consorzio Industriale del Lazio. The Strategic Development Plan of the Lazio ZLS (Simplified Logistics Zone) lists there manufacturers such as Johnson & Johnson, Procter & Gamble and Fiorucci, logistics operators such as DHL Supply Chain, GLS, STEF, Rifline and Cab Log, and large-scale retail distribution centres for SMA-Auchan, Coop, Carrefour and Pam Panorama.

The area is home to the Pomezia-Santa Palomba intermodal terminal. According to the regional mobility, transport and logistics plan (PRMTL), it is the largest in Lazio with about 60,000 intermodal transport units a year, has 6 intermodal and 5 conventional tracks on about 132,000 square metres, and is the main regional terminal for dangerous goods. The same Plan points out its limits: road access is possible only from the Pontina and the Ardeatina, the latter with one lane in each direction and heavy articulated lorry traffic, while on the rail side the crossing of the Rome node is a constraint.

On 5 March 2026 Euromerci reported that iDEA Group, through its development company iDEA SGV, had acquired a site in sector A of the Consorzio Industriale del Lazio for a new logistics park of about 23,650 square metres, a few kilometres from the Grande Raccordo Anulare (GRA, Rome's orbital motorway). The group had already developed about 80,000 square metres of logistics space there for a regional distribution operator.

## Pomezia and the Pontina

In [Pomezia]({{< relref "/logistica/pomezia" >}}) new warehouses are concentrated along the Pontina. On 27 October 2026 Il Giornale della Logistica reported that Logicor had started a courier cross-dock of about 7,101 square metres on a 19,307 square metre plot in Via Brasile, built to suit for SDA Express Courier of the Poste Italiane Group, with 90 bays for vans and 6 for heavy goods vehicles and BREEAM Excellent certification; delivery is expected in the first quarter of 2027. According to the publication, the site is 6 kilometres from the SS148 Pontina, 14 from the GRA and 35 from Fiumicino. Logicor has other developments in Pomezia in Via della Solfarata (over 37,000 square metres), Via Ponselè (20,000), the Naro area (24,500) and Campobello (14,200).

Pomezia is also a chemical-pharmaceutical cluster. The ZLS Plan cites ACS Dobfar, Alfasigma and Menarini Biotech; in July 2026 Alfasigma presented an investment plan of more than 40 million euros for 2026-2027 at its site, which employs more than 500 people. In [Ardea]({{< relref "/logistica/ardea" >}}), served by the Pontina and the Ardeatina, Amazon scheduled the opening of a distribution centre for spring 2022, with about 200 permanent hires in the first three years, according to TrasportoEuropa.

## Castel Romano, Ardeatina and Laurentina

The industrial area of [Castel Romano]({{< relref "/logistica/castel-romano" >}}), in the Municipality of Rome, covers 266 hectares, of which 191 industrial, at km 23 of the Pontina, according to the Consorzio Industriale del Lazio. Closer to the city, the [EUR]({{< relref "/logistica/eur" >}}) area and the districts crossed by the Laurentina and the Ardeatina are delivery destinations rather than locations for large warehouses. For the Ardeatina, the PRMTL documents congestion linked to heavy goods vehicles heading for Santa Palomba and the replacement of some signal-controlled junctions with roundabouts. No freight traffic data from a primary source is available for the Laurentina.

## Fiumicino

Leonardo da Vinci airport in [Fiumicino]({{< relref "/logistica/fiumicino" >}}) is Italy's largest airport for passengers, with more than 50 million travellers in 2025 according to ENAC data reported by Italpress; for cargo, Italy's largest airport is Milan Malpensa. The airport's Cargo City is an international gateway and a Core node of the TEN-T network, according to the ZLS Plan. Next to it is the Roma-Fiumicino logistics platform (CIRF), for which the PRMTL provides for the rail project in the medium term and the rail connection in the long term, with a role as an inland port for Civitavecchia along the route to Santa Palomba.

## Why companies also look to the province of Latina

Public sources offer some elements for comparing the southern quadrant of Rome with the province of Latina, without treating one as an extension of the other.

- **Industrial areas.** The Consorzio Industriale del Lazio counts in the province of Latina the industrial areas of Aprilia (256 hectares), Cisterna di Latina (368), Latina Scalo (448, across Latina, Sermoneta and Sezze), Pontinia (126) and Mazzocchio (445): areas comparable with those of Santa Palomba and Castel Romano.
- **ZLS and tax credit.** Of the 64 municipalities in the Lazio Simplified Logistics Zone, 15 are in the province of Latina; Aprilia (167 hectares), Cisterna di Latina (192) and part of Latina (280) are also in areas eligible for regional aid under Article 107(3)(c), a condition for the tax credit on capital goods, to be checked parcel by parcel.
- **Production chains.** According to the Frosinone-Latina Chamber of Commerce on ISTAT data, in 2025 the province of Latina exported pharmaceutical products worth 8.5 billion euros and ranks second in Italy for exports in the sector.
- **Access to the A1.** The Cisterna-Valmontone link road, under construction since October 2026, will connect the Pontina to the A1 without passing through the Rome node; it does not yet have an opening date.
- **Access to the Rome clusters.** The PRMTL documents the road access problems of Santa Palomba, linked to the Pontina and the Ardeatina.

The Rome quadrant retains the advantage of shorter distances from the GRA, the intermodal terminal and the airport: for Logicor, for example, the Via Brasile site is 14 kilometres from the GRA and 35 from Fiumicino.

## The Latina Scalo site

Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo, in Via della Dinamica, Latina Scalo, is about 49.8 kilometres by road from Pomezia (50 minutes by car), 54.8 from Santa Palomba, 57.1 from Castel Romano, 70.4 from EUR (70 minutes) and 89.5 from Fiumicino (86 minutes), according to the OSRM route calculation; journey times for heavy goods vehicles are longer. With 19,000 square metres of covered space, 25,000 square metres of open space and modular buildings from 500 to 3,000 square metres, the site offers [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}), [third-party logistics]({{< relref "/servizi/logistica-3pl" >}}) and [road distribution]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}) to the [southern quadrant of Rome]({{< relref "/logistica/roma-sud" >}}). Up-to-date distances are on the [location]({{< relref "/posizione" >}}) page.
