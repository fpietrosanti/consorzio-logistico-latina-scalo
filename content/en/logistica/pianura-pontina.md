Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo operates on Via della Dinamica, in the Tor Tre Ponti area of Latina Scalo (LT), with a hub of 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open areas. For businesses in the Pianura Pontina it is a third-party warehouse a short distance away: central Latina is about 8.4 km by road, Sermoneta about 10.6 km, Cisterna di Latina about 14.8 km, Sezze about 16.1 km and Pontinia about 17.1 km. Aprilia and Sabaudia are about 33.5 and 33.7 km away.

## Economic and logistics profile

The Pianura Pontina is the area reclaimed in the 1930s, between the Monti Lepini and the sea. It accounts for most of the population and industry of the province of Latina: Latina has about 127,000 inhabitants, Aprilia about 74,000, Cisterna di Latina about 36,000.

Industry is organised in the zones of the Consorzio Industriale del Lazio: Aprilia (256 hectares between Aprilia and Lanuvio), Cisterna di Latina (368 hectares), Latina Scalo (448 hectares across Latina, Sermoneta and Sezze) and Pontinia (126 hectares). For the Aprilia, Cisterna, Latina and Pontinia area, the ZLS Strategic Development Plan reports 40,286 local units and 150,336 employees (2022 data) and a specialisation in the manufacture of medicinal products and pharmaceutical preparations. For Cisterna and Latina the Plan cites companies such as Nuova Ompi (Stevanato Group), BSP Pharmaceuticals and Plasta Rei. In 2025 Latina was the second Italian province for pharmaceutical industry exports, according to the Frosinone-Latina Chamber of Commerce based on ISTAT (Italian National Institute of Statistics) data.

Agriculture is the second pillar. The Agro Pontino was among the first areas in Italy to host specialised kiwifruit orchards, in the 1970s; Kiwi Latina PGI, recognised by the European Union in 2004, is produced in municipalities of the provinces of Latina and Rome. The coastal belt, Sabaudia in particular, has a large greenhouse vegetable sector.

Six of the 15 Pontine municipalities in the Lazio ZLS (Simplified Logistics Zone) are in this area: Aprilia, Cisterna di Latina, Latina, Pontinia, Sermoneta and Sezze. In Pontinia, Sermoneta and Sezze the ZLS areas are not eligible for the tax credit, because they lie outside the Article 107(3)(c) areas of the regional aid map.

## Roads and rail

The SS148 Pontina crosses the area from Aprilia to Latina, as a dual carriageway as far as Borgo Piave, and continues towards Sabaudia and Terracina with one lane in each direction. The SS7 Appia connects Cisterna di Latina, Latina Scalo and Pontinia. The SS156 dei Monti Lepini runs from Latina towards Frosinone and the A1. Since October 2026 the Cisterna–Valmontone link road has been under construction, with junctions at Campoverde and Cisterna di Latina. Latina station, in Latina Scalo, is on the Rome–Formia–Naples line; the intermodal terminal at the former sugar refinery is not in operation, and the Region plans to reopen it for temperature-controlled supply chains.

## Flows to Latina Scalo

The short distances allow daily shuttles between plants and warehouse. From Aprilia, goods arrive via the Pontina; from Cisterna di Latina and Pontinia via the Appia; from Sezze, Sermoneta and Norma via the provincial roads at the foot of the Lepini; from Sabaudia and San Felice Circeo via the Pontina and the coastal provincial roads.

## Business requirements and services

For businesses in the area the hub serves as additional space a few kilometres from their plants. For [pharmaceutical]({{< relref "/settori/farmaceutico" >}}) and [manufacturing]({{< relref "/settori/manifatturiero" >}}) companies the hub offers [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}) of materials and finished products with recording in the WMS. For [agri-food]({{< relref "/settori/agroalimentare" >}}) there are temperature-controlled areas, [cross-docking]({{< relref "/servizi/cross-docking" >}}) to large retail chains and [value-added services]({{< relref "/servizi/lavorazioni-valore-aggiunto" >}}) such as labelling and palletising. [Transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}) with a dedicated fleet covers local and national B2B deliveries.

## Requesting a quotation

For a quotation, state the municipality and plant of origin, type of goods, format of the unit loads, number of pallets or square metres, duration of storage and shuttle frequency. Requests can be sent via the [contact form]({{< relref "/" >}}#contatti).
