## Road transport with a dedicated fleet

Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo carries out road freight transport with a dedicated fleet, from the Via della Dinamica site, Tor Tre Ponti area, Latina Scalo (LT). The service covers last-mile distribution and B2B deliveries across Italy, and can be contracted on its own or together with [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}) and [handling]({{< relref "/servizi/handling" >}}) at the same site.

When warehousing and transport are managed by the same operator, the order moves from preparation to loading without a change of contact: the transport document is issued at loading and the delivery is recorded in the same system that manages the stock.

## Road connections from the site

Latina Scalo can be reached from the SS148 Pontina and the SS7 Appia. The Pontina, managed by ANAS (the Italian state road operator), connects Rome to Terracina over about 109 km; between Rome and Borgo Piave (Latina) it is a dual carriageway with two lanes in each direction, while towards Terracina it continues with one lane in each direction. The SS156 Monti Lepini road connects Latina to Frosinone and the A1 motorway network. Since October 2026 the Cisterna–Valmontone link road has been under construction; it will connect the Pontina to the A1 near Valmontone. There is no confirmed opening date.

## Types of service

### Last-mile distribution

Last-mile distribution covers the final leg to retail outlets, construction sites, workshops and end customers. Delivery rounds are planned by zone and by the consignee's time window, with the aim of reducing empty running and unnecessary stops. The service is suited to distribution in the [province of Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/provincia-di-latina" >}}) and the southern districts of [Rome]({{< relref "/logistica/roma" >}}).

### B2B deliveries across Italy

For deliveries to companies outside the region, the Consortium organises full truckload shipments, when the volume fills the whole vehicle, or part loads. Shipments are planned according to destination, weight, volume and the receiving hours specified by the consignee.

### Warehouse-related transport

The fleet also carries out transfers linked to the warehouse cycle: collections from the client's plants, store replenishment, return of goods and returnable packaging. For flows that do not require storage, transport is combined with [cross-docking]({{< relref "/servizi/cross-docking" >}}).

## The shipment process

1. **Receipt of the transport order**, from the client's management system or with a loading document.
2. **Planning**: assignment of vehicle and round, check of delivery times and constraints.
3. **Loading** at the site's covered bays, with packages and pallets checked against the transport document (DDT, Italian delivery note).
4. **Delivery**, with the consignee's signature on the document and any reservations noted.
5. **Return of signed documents** and update of the shipment status, as described on the [document management]({{< relref "/servizi/gestione-documentale" >}}) page.

## Sectors served

Transport serves supply chains with different requirements: deliveries to site for [construction]({{< relref "/settori/edilizia" >}}), store replenishment for [large-scale retail]({{< relref "/settori/gdo-retail" >}}), components for [automotive]({{< relref "/settori/automotive" >}}) and [manufacturing]({{< relref "/settori/manifatturiero" >}}), parcels for [e-commerce]({{< relref "/settori/ecommerce" >}}). For the areas [south of Rome]({{< relref "/logistica/roma-sud" >}}), such as Pomezia and Santa Palomba, the site is about 50–55 km away by road.

## Requesting a quote

For a transport quote, please state collection and delivery locations, number of pallets or packages, weight and volume per shipment, weekly or monthly frequency, consignees' receiving hours and any constraints, such as delivery where no loading dock is available. Requests can be sent via the [contact form]({{< relref "/" >}}#contatti).
