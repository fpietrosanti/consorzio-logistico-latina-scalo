This page describes how **consorziologistico.it** uses cookies and similar technologies, under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), Article 122 of the Italian Privacy Code and the Italian Data Protection Authority's cookie guidelines of 10 June 2021.

## Data controller

Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo, Via della Dinamica snc, Zona Tor Tre Ponti, 04100 Latina Scalo (LT), Italy.
Email: [info@consorziologistico.it](mailto:info@consorziologistico.it) · PEC: consorziologisticolatinascalo@pec.it

## Technical storage (no consent needed)

To remember the choice made in the banner, the site stores the item `clls-consent` in your browser (localStorage), with the value "accepted" or "rejected". It contains no personal data, is never sent to any server and stays until you delete it from your browser.

## Statistics cookies: Google Analytics 4 (only with consent)

Only if you click "Accept" does the site load Google Analytics 4, a service of Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland), to measure in aggregate form how many people visit the site, which pages and languages, which sites they come from and which devices they use.

- Cookies set: `_ga` and `_ga_<ID>`, maximum duration 2 years.
- Google Signals and ad personalisation are disabled; no profiling or advertising use.
- Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.
- Data is kept in Google Analytics for 14 months.
- Google may process data in the United States; the transfer relies on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, to which Google LLC is certified.

Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). If you click "Reject", or make no choice, Google Analytics is not loaded and no data is sent to Google.

Google's privacy policy: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Third-party content

Some pages embed content from other providers, which receive the visitor's IP address in order to display it:

- typefaces from Google Fonts (Google);
- the map on the home page (Google Maps);
- the maps of the areas served (OpenStreetMap, with the Leaflet library served by unpkg.com).

## Changing or withdrawing consent

You can change your mind at any time with the <a href="#" data-cookie-prefs><strong>Cookie preferences</strong></a> link at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw consent, the site deletes the `_ga` cookies and no longer loads Google Analytics. You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser settings.

## Your rights

You can exercise your rights under Articles 15–22 GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection) by writing to [info@consorziologistico.it](mailto:info@consorziologistico.it), and lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority ([garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)).
