The glossary collects the terms most frequently found in logistics contracts, warehouse offers and transport documents. Entries follow Italian alphabetical order; each Italian headword is followed by its English equivalent in brackets, and for regulatory terms the source is given. Related articles explain the more complex topics: the [cost of a pallet space]({{< relref "/approfondimenti/costo-posto-pallet-come-si-calcola" >}}), the [third-party logistics contract]({{< relref "/approfondimenti/logistica-conto-terzi-pmi-contratto-kpi" >}}) and [transport documents]({{< relref "/approfondimenti/documenti-trasporto-merci-ddt-cmr" >}}).

## 0-9

**3PL (third-party logistics)**
: Logistics operator that manages activities such as storage, handling, order picking and transport on behalf of a client. In Italian: operatore logistico conto terzi.

**4PL (fourth-party logistics)**
: Party that coordinates several logistics operators and carriers on behalf of the client, managing the supply chain without directly carrying out the physical operations.

## A

**ABC (analisi) (ABC analysis)**
: Classification of items into three classes according to their contribution to movements or value: a few class A items generate most of the outbound volume. It is used to decide the position of locations and the frequency of stock counts.

**ADR (ADR Agreement)**
: European Agreement concerning the International Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road, signed in Geneva in 1957 and updated every two years. It sets out classification, packaging, labelling, documents and requirements for vehicles and drivers.

**Albo degli autotrasportatori (national register of road hauliers)**
: National register of companies carrying out road haulage of goods for hire. The registration number is one of the elements of the written contract of carriage required by Legislative Decree 286/2005.

**Altezza sotto trave (clear height)**
: Usable internal height of a warehouse, measured from the floor to the underside of the roof beams. It determines the number of racking levels and therefore the capacity in pallet spaces.

**ASN (Advance Shipping Notice)**
: Advance notice that the supplier sends to the warehouse with the contents, quantities and arrival time of a load. It is the prerequisite for fast receiving and cross-docking.

**Autoarticolato (articulated lorry)**
: Vehicle made up of a tractor unit and a semi-trailer. In Italian trade jargon it is also called bilico.

## B

**B2B / B2C**
: Business to business and business to consumer: they refer respectively to business customers and end consumers. The two kinds of logistics differ in order size, packaging and returns management.

**Backorder**
: Order, or order line, that cannot be fulfilled because of lack of availability and is held pending replenishment.

**Baia di carico (loading bay)**
: Point on the dock where the vehicle pulls up for loading and unloading, normally fitted with a door and a dock leveller to the vehicle bed. Bays are counted by the type of vehicle served.

**Banchina (loading dock)**
: Loading front of the warehouse, raised above the yard, onto which the loading bays open.

## C

**Carico completo (full truck load, FTL)**
: Shipment that occupies the whole vehicle and travels without intermediate stops from sender to consignee.

**Carico parziale (less than truck load, LTL)**
: Shipment that occupies only part of the vehicle, consolidated with goods from other senders.

**Carrello elevatore (forklift truck)**
: Self-propelled vehicle with forks for lifting and moving unit loads. The main variants are the counterbalance truck, the reach truck for narrow aisles and the turret truck for high-density racking.

**Catena del freddo (cold chain)**
: Maintenance of the prescribed temperature throughout all stages of storage and transport of perishable or temperature-sensitive products, with data recording.

**CeDi (centro di distribuzione; distribution centre)**
: Warehouse from which a large-scale retail chain supplies its stores.

**CMR**
: The 1956 Geneva Convention on the Contract for the International Carriage of Goods by Road and, by extension, the international consignment note deriving from it. It limits the carrier's liability to 8.33 special drawing rights per kilogram of gross weight.

**Collo (package, carton)**
: Single shipping unit, such as a box, crate or bundle, identified and handled as one piece.

**Committente (client, principal)**
: Party that entrusts goods and services to a logistics operator or carrier and pays for them.

**Contract logistics**
: Logistics entrusted to an operator under a multi-year contract governing services, service levels and rates. According to the Contract Logistics Observatory of the Politecnico di Milano, the average duration of warehousing contracts in Italy is about three years.

**Controstallie (demurrage)**
: Charge payable to the shipping line when a full container remains in the terminal beyond the free period (free time).

**Cross-docking**
: Operating method in which goods received are sorted and reloaded onto outgoing vehicles without being put into stock, normally within the day. See the article [cross-docking: when it makes sense]({{< relref "/approfondimenti/cross-docking-quando-conviene" >}}).

**Cut-off (cut-off time)**
: Latest time by which an order received by the warehouse is shipped the same day. It is calculated backwards from the carrier's collection time.

**Cycle counting (inventario rotativo; cycle counting)**
: Periodic count of part of the locations according to a schedule, so that every item is counted several times a year without stopping operations.

## D

**DDT (documento di trasporto; transport document, delivery note)**
: Tax document provided for by DPR 472/1996 that accompanies or records the movement of goods, with date, sequential number, parties and description of the goods. It allows deferred invoicing and proves transfers that do not transfer ownership, such as deposit with third parties.

**Deposito doganale (customs warehouse)**
: Warehouse authorised by the Customs Agency in which non-Union goods are held with import duties and VAT suspended, under the Union Customs Code (Regulation (EU) No 952/2013).

**Depositario (depositary, bailee)**
: Party that receives goods belonging to others for safekeeping, with the obligation to return them. The Italian Civil Code (Art. 1768) requires the diligence of a «buon padre di famiglia» (the reasonable person standard).

**Detention**
: Charge payable to the shipping line when the container remains outside the terminal, at the consignee's or warehouse's premises, beyond the agreed period.

**Dock-to-stock**
: Time between unloading the goods at the dock and their availability in the system for orders.

**Drive-in (scaffalatura) (drive-in racking)**
: Compact racking in which the forklift drives into the loading lanes; it offers high density but limited access to individual pallets.

## E

**e-CMR**
: International consignment note in electronic form, provided for by the 2008 Additional Protocol to the CMR. Italy acceded to it by Law No 37 of 8 March 2024.

**EAN / GTIN**
: GS1 numerical code that uniquely identifies a trade item; the most common format on consumer products is EAN-13.

**EPAL (pallet) (EPAL pallet, Euro pallet)**
: Wooden pallet measuring 1,200 × 800 millimetres produced to the specifications of the EPAL association and the UNI EN 13698-1 standard. It is the reference format in Europe and is exchanged within pallet pools.

**Evasione degli ordini (fulfillment) (order fulfilment)**
: All the activities from receipt of the order to dispatch: picking, checking, packing, labelling and handover to the carrier. In e-commerce it also includes returns management.

**EXW (Ex Works)**
: Incoterms 2020 rule under which the seller makes the goods available to the buyer at its own premises, without loading them; all subsequent costs and risks are borne by the buyer.

## F

**FEFO (First Expired, First Out)**
: Picking method under which the goods with the nearest expiry date go out first, regardless of the date of receipt. It is the standard for food, medicines and cosmetics.

**FIFO (First In, First Out)**
: Picking method under which the goods that came in first go out first. It limits the ageing of stock not subject to expiry.

**Filmatura (stretch-wrapping)**
: Wrapping of the unit load in stretch film to stabilise and protect it during handling and transport.

**Finestre orarie (slot) (time slots)**
: Booked time bands for loading or unloading a vehicle at a warehouse or distribution centre, used to reduce waiting times at the dock.

**Franchigia di sosta (free dwell time allowance)**
: Maximum free dwell time of goods in a transit or cross-docking area, beyond which the storage rate applies.

**Free time**
: Period of free use of a container granted by the shipping line, after which demurrage and detention are charged.

## G

**GDO (grande distribuzione organizzata; large-scale retail)**
: Retail sector organised in chains of stores, normally supplied through their own or third-party distribution centres.

**GDP (Good Distribution Practice)**
: European Union guidelines on good distribution practice of medicinal products for human use (2013/C 343/01). They govern storage, transport, temperature and traceability in the pharmaceutical chain.

**Giacenza media (average stock)**
: Average quantity held in the warehouse over a period, calculated on daily or weekly readings. It is one of the calculation bases for the storage rate.

**Groupage (collettame) (groupage, consolidated freight)**
: Shipment in which goods from several senders are consolidated on the same vehicle and distributed to their respective consignees.

## H

**HACCP**
: Food hygiene self-control system based on hazard analysis and critical control points, mandatory for food business operators under Regulation (EC) No 852/2004.

**Handling**
: All the operations of moving and manipulating goods in the warehouse: unloading, put-away, picking, loading.

## I

**Inbound / outbound**
: Flows into and out of the warehouse. In Italian: ricevimento e spedizione.

**Incoterms**
: International Chamber of Commerce rules that allocate the obligations, costs and risks of delivery between seller and buyer. The edition in use, Incoterms 2020, comprises eleven rules.

**Indice di rotazione (stock turnover ratio)**
: Ratio between outbound volume over a period, normally a year, and average stock. A ratio of 12 indicates an average stay of one month.

**Interporto (freight village, inland intermodal hub)**
: Intermodal logistics facility recognised under Law 240/1990, with a rail terminal, warehouses and services, connected to the main road network.

**Inventario (stocktake, stock count)**
: Physical count of stock, compared with the data in the information system. It can be annual, with operations suspended, or cyclical.

**ISPM 15**
: FAO international phytosanitary standard for wood packaging material in international trade, requiring heat treatment or fumigation and a compliance mark.

## K

**Kitting (composizione di kit) (kitting)**
: Process by which several items are combined into a single pack or sales unit.

**KPI (Key Performance Indicator)**
: Performance indicator with a defined formula, data source and threshold, used in logistics contracts to measure the service level.

## L

**Lavorazioni a valore aggiunto (value-added services)**
: Activities carried out in the warehouse in addition to storage, such as labelling, repacking, kitting and customisation.

**Lead time**
: Time between placing an order and delivery of the goods. In everyday Italian texts the term tempi di consegna is also used.

**Lettera di vettura (consignment note)**
: Document issued by the sender to the carrier stating the consignee, destination, and the nature, weight and quantity of the goods (Art. 1684 of the Civil Code); in international road transport it is the CMR.

**LIFO (Last In, First Out)**
: Picking method under which the goods that came in last go out first. It is used for non-perishable materials stored in block stacks or drive-in racking.

**Logistica conto terzi (third-party logistics)**
: Logistics carried out by an operator for clients other than itself. According to the Contract Logistics Observatory of the Politecnico di Milano, in Italy it is worth about 112 billion euros (2025 estimate).

**Lotto (batch, lot)**
: Set of units produced under the same conditions and identified by a common code, enabling traceability and recalls.

## M

**Magazzino dedicato (dedicated warehouse)**
: Warehouse, or area, reserved for a single client, with specific staff and processes.

**Magazzino multicliente (multi-user warehouse, shared-user warehouse)**
: Warehouse in which the operator serves several clients, sharing space, equipment and staff.

**Mezzanino (mezzanine)**
: Raised floor inside the warehouse used for offices, processing or storage of light items.

**Milk run**
: Scheduled collection or delivery round that calls at several suppliers or customers in a fixed sequence with the same vehicle.

**Movimentazione (materials handling)**
: Physical movement of goods within the warehouse or between the warehouse and transport vehicles.

## O

**Operatore logistico (logistics operator, logistics service provider)**
: Company that provides logistics services on behalf of clients: storage, handling, transport, value-added services and document management.

**OTIF (On Time In Full)**
: Indicator measuring the percentage of on-time and complete deliveries out of total deliveries in the period.

## P

**Packing (imballo) (packing)**
: Stage in which picked goods are checked, packed and prepared for dispatch.

**Pallet**
: Platform, normally of wood or plastic, on which a unit load is built so that it can be moved with forklifts and pallet trucks.

**Pallettizzazione (palletising)**
: Building loose goods or cartons onto a pallet, with stretch-wrapping or strapping, to form a stable unit load.

**Picking (prelievo) (order picking)**
: Retrieval of the units required by an order from their locations. It can be by full pallet, by carton or by item.

**Piattaforma distributiva (distribution platform)**
: Warehouse from which deliveries to stores or customers depart, often with cross-docking flows.

**Piazzale (yard)**
: Paved external area of the logistics park, used for manoeuvring and parking vehicles and for storing goods suitable for outdoor storage.

**POD (Proof of Delivery)**
: Proof of delivery, on paper or electronic, signed by the consignee. It is the data source for punctuality indicators.

**Polo logistico (logistics park)**
: Site with one or more warehouses used for logistics, with the related yards and services.

**Portata del pavimento (floor load capacity)**
: Maximum load permitted by the industrial floor, expressed in tonnes per square metre.

**Posto pallet (pallet space, pallet position)**
: Location, in racking or on the floor, intended for one palletised unit load within height and weight limits. It is the most widely used charging unit in third-party storage.

**PRSES (person responsible for storage equipment safety)**
: Person responsible for the safety of racking, appointed by the employer under the UNI EN 15635 standard, who organises periodic inspections at least once a year.

## R

**Reach stacker**
: Self-propelled vehicle with a telescopic boom for lifting and stacking containers in the yard.

**Ricevimento merci (goods receiving)**
: Stage of unloading, quantity and quality checking and taking charge of incoming goods, with registration in the warehouse system.

**Riconfezionamento (co-packing) (repacking, co-packing)**
: Remaking or modifying sales packs, for example for promotions or different markets.

**Riga d'ordine (order line)**
: Single item with its quantity within an order. It is the unit of measurement of picking activity.

**Rottura di carico (break-bulk, transshipment)**
: Transfer of goods from one vehicle to another, usually from a heavy vehicle to smaller vehicles for final distribution.

## S

**Saturazione (occupancy rate, capacity utilisation)**
: Ratio between occupied pallet spaces and available spaces in a warehouse. Occupancy consistently close to the maximum reduces operational flexibility.

**Scaffalatura porta-pallet (pallet racking)**
: Steel structure with uprights and beams that holds palletised unit loads on several levels, each individually accessible.

**Scheda di trasporto (transport sheet)**
: Document introduced in 2008 for road haulage for hire and abolished by Law 190/2014 (2015 Stability Law), Art. 1(247).

**Scorta di sicurezza (safety stock)**
: Quantity of goods held in the warehouse to absorb variations in demand or supply delays.

**SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)**
: Code identifying a single item held in stock, distinguished by characteristics such as size, colour or format.

**SLA (Service Level Agreement)**
: Contract annex defining the expected service levels, the indicators that measure them and any penalties.

**SSCC (Serial Shipping Container Code)**
: 18-digit GS1 code that uniquely identifies a logistics unit, such as a pallet or carton, along the supply chain.

**Stoccaggio a terra (floor storage, block stacking)**
: Storage of unit loads on the floor, single or stacked, without racking.

## T

**TEU (Twenty-foot Equivalent Unit)**
: Unit of measurement of container capacity and traffic, equal to one 20-foot container. A 40-foot container counts as two TEU.

**Terminal intermodale (intermodal terminal)**
: Facility for transferring intermodal transport units between road and rail. In Lazio the main one is the Pomezia–Santa Palomba terminal.

**TMS (Transportation Management System)**
: Transport management software: route planning, carrier selection, tracking and cost control.

**Tracciabilità (traceability)**
: Ability to reconstruct the path of a batch or unit along the supply chain, from origin to delivery.

**Transit time**
: Travel time of the goods from collection to delivery, excluding preparation time in the warehouse.

**Transpallet (pallet truck)**
: Manual or electric device for lifting pallets a few centimetres off the floor and moving them.

## U

**Ubicazione (storage location, bin location)**
: Physical position identified by a code (aisle, bay, level) in which the warehouse system records the goods.

**UDC (unità di carico; unit load)**
: Goods grouped together to be handled as a single unit, such as a stretch-wrapped pallet or a roll container.

**Ultimo miglio (last mile)**
: Final leg of delivery to the consignee, often in an urban area and subject to access restrictions. See the article on the [last mile to south Rome]({{< relref "/approfondimenti/ultimo-miglio-roma-da-latina" >}}).

**UTI (unità di trasporto intermodale; intermodal transport unit, ITU)**
: Container, swap body or semi-trailer that can be transferred between different transport modes without break-bulk.

## V

**Vettore (carrier)**
: Company that undertakes to carry goods from one place to another for payment (Art. 1678 of the Civil Code).

**VMI (Vendor Managed Inventory)**
: Model in which the supplier manages stock at the customer's premises and decides on replenishment on the basis of consumption data.

## W

**Wave picking (prelievo a ondate) (wave picking)**
: Organisation of picking in waves of orders grouped by departure time, carrier or destination area.

**WMS (Warehouse Management System)**
: Warehouse management software that records locations, stock, batches and movements and directs receiving, picking and dispatch operations.

## Z

**ZES Unica (Single Special Economic Zone)**
: Special Economic Zone for southern Italy (Decree-Law 124/2023), comprising Abruzzo, Basilicata, Calabria, Campania, Molise, Puglia, Sicily and Sardinia and, since November 2025, Marche and Umbria. Lazio is not part of it.

**ZLS (Zona Logistica Semplificata; Simplified Logistics Zone)**
: Area with simplified administrative procedures and, in areas eligible for regional aid, a tax credit for investment in capital goods (Law 205/2017). The Lazio ZLS, established by Prime Ministerial Decree of 17 November 2025, comprises 64 municipalities, 15 of them in the province of Latina.

**ZTL (zona a traffico limitato; limited traffic zone)**
: Urban area with access regulated by time bands and vehicle categories. In Rome, goods vehicles are subject to the specific rules of the goods vehicle ZTL (ZTL Merci) and the VAM ZTL.
