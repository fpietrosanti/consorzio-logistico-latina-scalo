Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo serves businesses in the municipalities of Rome South from its hub on Via della Dinamica, in the Tor Tre Ponti area of Latina Scalo (LT), which comprises 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open areas. In this section, Rome South refers to the municipalities of the Metropolitan City of Rome to the south and south-east of the capital, grouped into three areas: the [Roman coast]({{< relref "/logistica/litorale-romano" >}}), the [Castelli Romani]({{< relref "/logistica/castelli-romani" >}}) and the [Valle del Sacco and Prenestina area]({{< relref "/logistica/valle-del-sacco-area-prenestina" >}}). The districts of the Municipality of Rome are covered on the [Rome]({{< relref "/logistica/roma" >}}) page.

## Economic and logistics profile

Santa Palomba is one of the main logistics hubs in Lazio. The Santa Palomba zone of the Consorzio Industriale del Lazio, between Rome and Pomezia, covers 483 hectares and is the largest in the Rome-Latina area. The ZLS Strategic Development Plan lists multinationals such as Johnson & Johnson and Procter & Gamble there, logistics operators such as DHL Supply Chain, GLS and STEF, and large retail chain platforms run by third parties. Pomezia is also a chemical and pharmaceutical cluster; the trade press describes the area as one of the growing logistics markets in Italy, with new developments in Pomezia and Ardea.

The second hub is the Valle del Sacco, along the A1 Rome–Naples motorway. In Colleferro, home to the aerospace industry (Avio), the ZLS Plan places SLIM, an integrated multimodal logistics system that has been partly built, between the Cisterna–Valmontone corridor, the A1 and the Rome–Cassino railway line. Trade publications cite distribution centres of Unieuro, GXO, GLP and Amazon in the area.

The Castelli Romani are crossed by the SS7 Appia and served by the provincial network that climbs from the lowland municipalities to the hill towns. The coast includes Anzio, with the port from which fast ferries leave for Ponza, and Fiumicino, with the Cargo City of Leonardo da Vinci airport, Italy's busiest airport by passengers.

## Infrastructure

The route connecting to Latina Scalo is the SS148 Pontina, a dual carriageway between Rome and Borgo Piave. The SS7 Appia links the Castelli Romani to Velletri and Cisterna di Latina. The Pomezia–Santa Palomba intermodal terminal, on the Rome–Naples line via Formia, ranks first in Lazio by intermodal transport units handled and is a Core node of the TEN-T network. Since October 2026 the Cisterna–Valmontone link road has been under construction, a toll-free main non-urban road with junctions at Campoverde, Cisterna di Latina, Lariano and Labico; until it opens, connections between the Valle del Sacco and Latina Scalo use ordinary roads.

## Flows to Latina Scalo

From the coast, goods reach Latina Scalo via the Pontina: Pomezia is about 49.8 km away by road, Ardea about 44.1 km. Anzio and Nettuno, at about 34 and 32 km, are connected by the provincial network. From the Castelli Romani the route is the Appia: Velletri is about 25.1 km away, Albano Laziale about 42.8 km, Frascati about 50.3 km. From the Valle del Sacco, routes descend via Artena and Cisterna: Valmontone is about 39.9 km away, Colleferro about 42.4 km.

## Business requirements and services

For companies in Rome South, the Pontine hub offers space some fifty kilometres from the Pomezia and Santa Palomba district. The hub is suited to [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}) of safety or seasonal stock away from the production site, to storage on [open yards]({{< relref "/servizi/piazzali-scoperti" >}}) of materials for [construction]({{< relref "/settori/edilizia" >}}) and [manufacturing]({{< relref "/settori/manifatturiero" >}}), to [value-added services]({{< relref "/servizi/lavorazioni-valore-aggiunto" >}}) on products destined for [grocery and retail chains]({{< relref "/settori/gdo-retail" >}}), and to [third-party logistics]({{< relref "/servizi/logistica-3pl" >}}) for [e-commerce]({{< relref "/settori/ecommerce" >}}). [Transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}) with a dedicated fleet connects the warehouse to customers in the area and to the national network.

## Requesting a quotation

For a quotation, state the municipality of origin or destination, type of goods, number of pallets or square metres required, expected duration, frequency of collections and deliveries, and additional services. Requests can be sent via the [contact form]({{< relref "/" >}}#contatti).
