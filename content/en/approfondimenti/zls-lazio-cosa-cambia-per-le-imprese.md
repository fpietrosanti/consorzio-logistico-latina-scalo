The Lazio Simplified Logistics Zone (ZLS, Zona Logistica Semplificata), established by Prime Ministerial Decree (DPCM) of 17 November 2025 following the Strategic Development Plan approved by the regional government with DGR No 291 of 29 April 2025, comprises 64 municipalities and about 4,026 hectares, according to the Plan itself: 16 municipalities in the province of Rome, 22 in Frosinone, 15 in Latina, 7 in Rieti and 4 in Viterbo. The port areas of Civitavecchia, Fiumicino and Gaeta are also included. For companies in the [province of Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/provincia-di-latina" >}}) the regime offers two separate instruments, a tax credit on investment in capital goods and a simplified authorisation procedure, but access to both depends on the cadastral parcel on which the facility stands.

## Legal framework and governance

ZLSs are provided for by Art. 1(61)–(65-bis) of Law No 205 of 27 December 2017 and governed by the regulation in DPCM No 40 of 4 March 2024. The Lazio boundaries went through a first version covering 49 municipalities (DGR 797/2024), later superseded by DGR 291/2025 and formalised by executive decree G13120 of 11 October 2025. A DPCM of 3 March 2026 established the Steering Committee, which held its first meeting on 4 June 2026 according to the minutes published by the Region. It is chaired by the President of the Region and its members include, among others, the Port System Authority of the Central-Northern Tyrrhenian Sea, the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport, the Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy, the Consorzio Industriale del Lazio and the competent Chamber of Commerce; provinces and municipalities take part as observers. The Committee's rules of procedure were published in August 2026.

At the first meeting the regional councillor for the budget, **Giancarlo Righini**, reported that the budget amendment approved at the end of May 2026 had created a commissioner for implementing the ZLS, tasked with speeding up investments and procedures.

The most relevant figure for logistics facilities concerns the provincial capital. The Municipality of [Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/latina" >}}) is in the ZLS for 280 hectares, but only in areas defined by cadastral sheet and parcel. Annex 1 to DGR 291/2025 (cadastral analysis) lists for Latina the Sicamb area, the Latina Scalo area (sheets 63, 64, 113 and 114, with 8 parcels), the Fulgor Cavi, Pozzi Ginori, F2 Piave, F2 Epitaffio and F1 S.S.156 areas and the capital's PIP (productive settlement plan) area. Some parcels in the F1 S.S.156 area are not included in the 107(3)(c) area.

## The 2026-2028 tax credit

The ZLS tax credit is governed by Art. 13 of Decree-Law No 60 of 7 May 2024, converted by Law 95/2024, and was extended to investments made in 2026, 2027 and 2028 by Law No 199 of 30 December 2025 (2026 Budget Law), Art. 1(444)–(447), with a spending cap of 100 million euros a year for all Italian ZLSs.

Italian Revenue Agency (Agenzia delle Entrate) measure prot. 3873/2026, which approves the notification forms, sets the scope of the incentive: expenditure on the acquisition of capital goods for existing or new production facilities in the ZLSs, «limitatamente alle zone ammissibili agli aiuti a finalità regionale» (limited to the areas eligible for regional aid) identified by the 2022-2027 Regional Aid Map. The aid intensity is the maximum allowed by the Map for the individual area; there is therefore no single percentage valid for the whole territory. The share actually available also depends on the ratio between the claims submitted and the annual spending cap, determined by the measure provided for in paragraph 447.

Two technical conditions in the measure weigh on companies with several sites. The notification is rejected if the applicant does not have an active VAT number or if the ATECO 2025 activity code and the municipal cadastral code stated for the production facility do not match those notified to the Agency. The expenditure declared must then be certified by the company's statutory auditor or by another registered auditor.

## Compliance calendar

According to the measure, two electronic notifications are required each year, to be sent with the Agency's free software:

- the **notification** of expenditure incurred and planned in the year, from 31 March to 30 May (for 2026 the window has closed; for 2027 it runs from 31 March to 30 May 2027);
- the **supplementary notification**, mandatory on pain of forfeiture, certifying that the investments were made by 31 December: from 3 to 17 January of the following year (for 2026 investments, from 3 to 17 January 2027).

The credit can only be used by offsetting through the F24 payment form, from the working day following publication of the measure setting the applicable percentage and after a second receipt from the Agency acknowledging its use.

## Simplification and the SUAP

DPCM 40/2024 and the Strategic Development Plan provide for simplified administrative procedures and a one-stop shop for productive activities (SUAP) dedicated to the ZLS as the lead authority, with a single authorisation and standard-form settlement agreements. At the Committee's first meeting, the Vice-President of the Region, **Roberta Angelilli**, described the model as «uno Sportello unificato con un unico punto d'accesso» (a unified desk with a single point of access). For a logistics company, this means submitting in one place the building, environmental and operating applications connected with a new warehouse or an extension in a ZLS area.

According to the legislation consulted, the ZLS does not, on the other hand, provide preferential access to regional calls for funding or hiring incentives: the hiring bonus applies to the Single ZES, of which Lazio is not part.

## Checking the parcel, step by step

The cadastral criterion makes a preliminary check necessary for every site:

1. obtain the sheet and parcel numbers from the land registry extract for the building or land;
2. look them up in the municipality's section of Annex 1 to DGR 291/2025 (for Latina, section "36. Comune di Latina");
3. check that the parcel is shown as included and "Compresa" (within) the TFEU 107(3)(c) area;
4. align the ATECO 2025 code and the municipal cadastral code notified to the Revenue Agency for that facility;
5. schedule the notification in the 31 March–30 May window and plan for the auditor's certification for the January supplementary notification.

A company located in Pontinia, Sermoneta or Sezze may be in the ZLS and have access to the simplified procedures, but not to the tax credit, because these municipalities are not in a 107(3)(c) area. In Latina, the same street may contain both included and excluded parcels.

## A different regime: customs free zones

Law 199/2025 (Art. 1(453)) also provided for the establishment of enclosed customs free zones in Lazio, under the Union Customs Code (Regulation (EU) No 952/2013). With a notice published in April 2026 (DD G04864), the Region collected expressions of interest for the areas under its jurisdiction in zones LAZ3, LAZ4 and LAZ5-7 of the Regional Aid Map; the boundaries must be approved by the Customs and Monopolies Agency. Once the boundaries are set, the regime allows non-Union goods to be stored with import duties and VAT suspended. It is an instrument separate from the ZLS, with its own requirements.

## The Latina Scalo site

Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo operates in Via della Dinamica, in the Tor Tre Ponti area, with 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open space used for [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}) and [third-party logistics]({{< relref "/servizi/logistica-3pl" >}}). The Municipality of Latina is one of the 15 Pontine municipalities in the ZLS, but the inclusion of the Via della Dinamica plot has not yet been verified by sheet and parcel: for this reason the Consorzio does not describe its site as a ZLS area. Companies considering a facility or a logistics contract in the area can find data on the [location]({{< relref "/posizione" >}}) and on distances from the main roads.
