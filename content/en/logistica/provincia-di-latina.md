Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo provides businesses in the province of Latina with third-party storage, handling and transport from its hub on Via della Dinamica, in the Tor Tre Ponti area of Latina Scalo (LT). The site comprises 19,000 square metres of covered space, in modular warehouses of 500 to 3,000 square metres with clear heights of up to 10 metres, and 25,000 square metres of open areas. The province has 33 municipalities, divided in this section into four areas: [Pianura Pontina]({{< relref "/logistica/pianura-pontina" >}}), [Monti Lepini and Ausoni]({{< relref "/logistica/lepini-ausoni" >}}), [Sud Pontino and Gulf of Gaeta]({{< relref "/logistica/sud-pontino-golfo-di-gaeta" >}}) and [Pontine Islands]({{< relref "/logistica/isole-ponziane" >}}).

## Economic and logistics profile

The province of Latina has an industrial base concentrated in the zones of the Consorzio Industriale del Lazio, formed by the merger of five industrial consortia and with its operational headquarters in Latina, in the Tor Tre Ponti area. The Pontine industrial zones are Aprilia (256 hectares, between Aprilia and Lanuvio), Cisterna di Latina (368 hectares), Latina Scalo (448 hectares, across Latina, Sermoneta and Sezze), Pontinia (126 hectares) and Mazzocchio (445 hectares). In the south of the province, the former Consorzio Sud Pontino manages areas at Formia, Minturno, Gaeta, Santi Cosma e Damiano and Fondi.

The largest sector is pharmaceuticals. According to analysis by the Frosinone-Latina Chamber of Commerce based on ISTAT (Italian National Institute of Statistics) data, in 2025 Latina was the second Italian province for pharmaceutical industry exports; together with Frosinone it generates more than half of Lazio's exports. The ZLS Strategic Development Plan identifies the manufacture of medicinal products and pharmaceutical preparations as the specialisation of the Aprilia, Cisterna and Latina cluster, and lists road freight transport among its relevant activities.

The second sector is agri-food. The Agro Pontino was among the first areas in Italy to host specialised kiwifruit orchards, in the 1970s, and Kiwi Latina PGI has been recognised by the European Union since 2004. Fondi is home to the Centro Agroalimentare all'Ingrosso (MOF), an agri-food market of national interest which, according to its operator, handles almost 10 million quintals (about 1 million tonnes) of goods a year.

## Infrastructure

The SS148 Pontina, managed by ANAS (the Italian national road operator), links Rome to Terracina over about 109 km; between Rome and Borgo Piave it is a dual carriageway with two lanes in each direction, further south a single carriageway with one lane in each direction. The SS7 Appia runs through Cisterna, Latina Scalo, Terracina, Fondi and Formia. The SS156 dei Monti Lepini links Latina to Frosinone and the A1 motorway network. Since October 2026 the Cisterna–Valmontone link road, which will connect the Pontina to the A1, has been under construction; the Rome–Latina motorway project is under review.

By rail, Latina station is in Latina Scalo, on the Rome–Formia–Naples line. The intermodal terminal at the former sugar refinery is not in operation; the regional mobility, transport and logistics plan provides for its reopening as a terminal for temperature-controlled supply chains. The nearest intermodal terminal in operation is Pomezia–Santa Palomba. The province's port is Gaeta, part of the Port System Authority of the Central-Northern Tyrrhenian Sea, which handled about 1.8 million tonnes of goods in 2025, mainly bulk cargo.

## Flows to Latina Scalo

From the northern belt of the province, goods reach Latina Scalo via the Pontina (Aprilia, about 33.5 km by road) or the Appia (Cisterna di Latina, about 14.8 km). From the south the route is the Appia: Terracina is about 38.2 km away, Fondi about 56.3 km, Formia about 73.8 km. For the Gulf municipalities near the Campania border, such as Minturno and Santi Cosma e Damiano, the routing calculation gives the route via the A1 and SS156 as the fastest. The Lepini municipalities are connected by the SS156 and the provincial road network.

## Business requirements and services

Pharmaceutical and chemical companies in the area can entrust an external warehouse with stocks of packaging materials and finished products awaiting shipment, with requirements to be verified case by case: the [pharmaceutical sector]({{< relref "/settori/farmaceutico" >}}) has a dedicated page. [Agri-food]({{< relref "/settori/agroalimentare" >}}) businesses have seasonal peaks that require temporary [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}), including in the hub's temperature-controlled areas, and [cross-docking]({{< relref "/servizi/cross-docking" >}}) to large retail chains. For [manufacturing]({{< relref "/settori/manifatturiero" >}}) and [construction]({{< relref "/settori/edilizia" >}}) the [open yards]({{< relref "/servizi/piazzali-scoperti" >}}) are available. [Transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}) with a dedicated fleet covers last-mile distribution within the province and national B2B deliveries.

## Requesting a quotation

For a quotation, state the municipality of origin or destination, type of goods, format of the unit loads, number of pallets or square metres, duration of storage and additional services. Requests can be sent via the [contact form]({{< relref "/" >}}#contatti).
