Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo serves businesses in the southern quadrant of the Municipality of Rome from its hub on Via della Dinamica, in the Tor Tre Ponti area of Latina Scalo (LT), with 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open areas. This page covers 32 localities between the Tiber and the A1, from Ostiense to Ostia and from Cinecittà to Tor Vergata. The closest to the hub are Morena, about 53.3 km by road, Santa Palomba about 54.8 km, Capannelle about 56.9 km and Castel Romano about 57.1 km; EUR is about 70.4 km away. The overview for the city is on the [Rome]({{< relref "/logistica/roma" >}}) page.

## Economic and logistics profile

The quadrant can be read as three bands. The first is the Pontina and Laurentina corridor, from EUR, Torrino and Spinaceto to Trigoria, Castel Romano and Santa Palomba. It contains two zones of the Consorzio Industriale del Lazio: Castel Romano, at km 23 of the Pontina, covering 266 hectares, and Santa Palomba, covering 483 hectares, straddling the border with Pomezia. Santa Palomba is one of the main logistics hubs in Lazio: according to the ZLS Strategic Development Plan, logistics operators such as DHL Supply Chain, GLS, STEF, Rifline and Cab Log and large retail chain platforms run by third parties operate there. The Pomezia–Santa Palomba intermodal terminal ranks first in Lazio by intermodal transport units handled.

The second band lies to the west, between Ostiense, Magliana, Ponte Galeria, Acilia and Ostia, oriented towards the Rome–Fiumicino motorway, the Cargo City of Leonardo da Vinci airport and the Rome–Fiumicino logistics platform. The third lies to the east, between Cinecittà, Anagnina, Capannelle, Morena, Romanina, Tor Vergata and Tor Bella Monaca, served by the Grande Raccordo Anulare, Via Tuscolana, the Appia and the A1 Rome South branch, with Ciampino airport a short distance away.

## Roads and rail

The main link to Latina Scalo is the SS148 Pontina, managed by ANAS (the Italian national road operator), which starts at EUR and is a dual carriageway with two lanes in each direction as far as Borgo Piave. The Rome–Latina motorway project is under review and has no opening date. For localities in the east of the quadrant, the alternative is the SS7 Appia, through the Castelli Romani, Velletri and Cisterna di Latina. The Pomezia–Santa Palomba terminal is on the Rome–Naples line via Formia, the same line that serves Latina station in Latina Scalo.

## Flows to Latina Scalo

According to the routing calculation, from localities along the Pontina the route is direct: Castel Romano is about 56 minutes away by car, Spinaceto about 65, Torrino about 68. On the western side journey times increase: Magliana about 73 minutes, Ponte Galeria about 77, Ostia about 94. From localities to the east the calculation uses the Appia or the A1 branch with the provincial road network: Morena about 70 minutes, Anagnina about 75, Tor Vergata about 75, Cinecittà about 78. Journey times are longer for heavy goods vehicles, particularly at peak times on the Grande Raccordo Anulare and the Pontina.

## Business requirements and services

For businesses in the quadrant, the Pontine hub offers space outside the city with modular warehouses of 500 to 3,000 square metres. The main uses are:

- [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}) of reserve stock and seasonal goods for retail and [large retail chains]({{< relref "/settori/gdo-retail" >}}), with scheduled replenishment of urban warehouses;
- [third-party logistics]({{< relref "/servizi/logistica-3pl" >}}) for [e-commerce]({{< relref "/settori/ecommerce" >}}) and [textiles and fashion]({{< relref "/settori/tessile-moda" >}}), with WMS and [document management]({{< relref "/servizi/gestione-documentale" >}});
- [cross-docking]({{< relref "/servizi/cross-docking" >}}) and [transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}) for flows to the province of Latina and southern Italy;
- [open yards]({{< relref "/servizi/piazzali-scoperti" >}}) for [construction]({{< relref "/settori/edilizia" >}}) and [manufacturing]({{< relref "/settori/manifatturiero" >}}) businesses.

## Requesting a quotation

For a quotation, state the locality of collection and delivery, type of goods, number of pallets or square metres required, duration of storage, frequency of deliveries in the city and additional services. Requests can be sent via the [contact form]({{< relref "/" >}}#contatti).
