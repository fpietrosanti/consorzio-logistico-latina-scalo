Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo serves businesses on the Roman coast (litorale romano) from its hub on Via della Dinamica, in the Tor Tre Ponti area of Latina Scalo (LT), with 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open areas. The area comprises five municipalities of the Metropolitan City of Rome: Nettuno, about 32.4 km by road from the hub, Anzio about 34.4 km, Ardea about 44.1 km, Pomezia about 49.8 km and Fiumicino about 89.5 km. Together they have more than 300,000 inhabitants.

## Economic and logistics profile

Pomezia is the area's main production and logistics hub. The trade press describes a growing logistics district, with new property developments along the SS148 Pontina; Pomezia is also a chemical and pharmaceutical cluster, with companies such as ACS Dobfar, Alfasigma and Menarini Biotech cited in the Lazio ZLS Strategic Development Plan. Straddling the border between Pomezia and Rome lies the 483-hectare Santa Palomba zone of the Consorzio Industriale del Lazio, where multinationals such as Johnson & Johnson and Procter & Gamble, logistics operators such as DHL Supply Chain, GLS and STEF, and large retail chain platforms run by third parties operate.

Ardea, served by the Pontina and Via Ardeatina, is identified by TrasportoEuropa as the site of new large-scale logistics developments serving central and southern Italy. Anzio and Nettuno have economies linked to the port, fishing and tourism; fast craft for [Ponza]({{< relref "/logistica/isole-ponziane" >}}) depart from the port of Anzio.

Fiumicino is home to Leonardo da Vinci airport, Italy's busiest airport by passengers, with its Cargo City, and the Rome–Fiumicino logistics platform, designed for road, rail, sea and air traffic; its rail terminal is under study. The port of Fiumicino is part of the Port System Authority of the Central-Northern Tyrrhenian Sea together with Civitavecchia and Gaeta.

## Roads and rail

The area's main route is the SS148 Pontina, managed by ANAS (the Italian national road operator), which links Rome to Latina as a dual carriageway with two lanes in each direction as far as Borgo Piave. Anzio and Nettuno are connected to Latina Scalo by the provincial network, including the SP38 and SP106b. Fiumicino is reached via the Pontina, the Grande Raccordo Anulare and the Rome–Fiumicino motorway. The Pomezia–Santa Palomba intermodal terminal, on the Rome–Naples line via Formia, ranks first in Lazio by intermodal transport units handled and is the main regional terminal for dangerous goods.

## Flows to Latina Scalo

From Pomezia and Ardea, flows follow the Pontina southwards, through Aprilia. From Anzio and Nettuno the fastest route uses the provincial roads towards Cisterna and Latina. Latina Scalo lies about 50 km south of the Pomezia district: for coastal businesses it is a separate logistics area, useful for additional stock or for flows heading to the province of Latina and the Sud Pontino.

## Business requirements and services

Coastal businesses can use the hub for:

- [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}) of stock for which there is no room in Pomezia warehouses or at the plants, in warehouses of 500 to 3,000 square metres;
- storage on [open yards]({{< relref "/servizi/piazzali-scoperti" >}}) of materials for [construction]({{< relref "/settori/edilizia" >}}) and [manufacturing]({{< relref "/settori/manifatturiero" >}});
- [cross-docking]({{< relref "/servizi/cross-docking" >}}) and [transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}) for distribution to the province of Latina and the Gulf of Gaeta;
- [third-party logistics]({{< relref "/servizi/logistica-3pl" >}}) for [e-commerce]({{< relref "/settori/ecommerce" >}}) and [large retail chains]({{< relref "/settori/gdo-retail" >}}), with WMS and [document management]({{< relref "/servizi/gestione-documentale" >}}).

For [pharmaceuticals]({{< relref "/settori/farmaceutico" >}}), storage conditions and the required authorisations are verified with the consortium case by case.

## Requesting a quotation

For a quotation, state the municipality and site of collection or delivery, type of goods, number of pallets or square metres required, duration of storage, shuttle frequency and additional services. Requests can be sent via the [contact form]({{< relref "/" >}}#contatti).
