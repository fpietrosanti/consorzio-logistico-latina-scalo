## Third-party storage on 19,000 square metres of covered space

Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo offers third-party goods storage at the Via della Dinamica site, Tor Tre Ponti area, Latina Scalo (LT). The covered area is 19,000 square metres, divided into modular buildings of 500 to 3,000 square metres each, with clear height up to 10 metres and prepared for pallet racking. Outside, 25,000 square metres of open space are available, described on the [open yards]({{< relref "/servizi/piazzali-scoperti" >}}) page.

The modular buildings allow the client to be assigned part of a multi-client warehouse or a dedicated building, depending on volumes and contract duration. The service is aimed at manufacturers, distributors and importers in the [province of Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/provincia-di-latina" >}}) and the southern districts of Rome that need additional space without leasing a property.

## Types of storage

### Racked storage

Palletised goods are placed on industrial pallet racking. The clear height of up to 10 metres allows capacity to be developed vertically. The number of levels and the bay configuration are set at the design stage, based on the dimensions and weight of the load units.

### Floor storage

Units not suited to racking, such as non-standard packages, reels or bulky packaging, are stored on the floor in marked areas. Stackability and stack heights are agreed with the client on the basis of the packaging specifications.

### Temperature-controlled areas

The site includes temperature-controlled storage areas. Conditions of use, including temperature ranges, must be checked with the Consortium for each type of goods before the contract is signed.

## The operating process

### Goods receipt

Incoming vehicles dock at the covered loading and unloading bays; the yards allow articulated lorries to manoeuvre and waiting vehicles to park. On receipt, the operator checks the goods against the transport document (DDT, Italian delivery note) and the advance shipping notice sent by the client, counts the packages, checks the condition of the packaging and notes any reservations on the carrier's document.

### Booking in and put-away

After the check, the goods are recorded in the WMS (warehouse management system) and put away in a location. Each load unit remains linked to the item code, the quantity and, where the client requires it, the batch. Put-away rules and picking logic, for example by date of receipt or by expiry date, are defined at start-up.

### Inventory and stock control

Stock recorded in the WMS is compared with physical counts according to the agreed schedule: cycle counts by item family or a full count at the end of the period. Any discrepancies are reported to the client with item and location details.

### Order picking and dispatch

On the client's order, the warehouse picks the goods, prepares the outbound units and hands them over to the designated carrier or to the Consortium's fleet. Handling activities are described on the [handling]({{< relref "/servizi/handling" >}}) page; packaging and labelling activities on the [value-added services]({{< relref "/servizi/lavorazioni-valore-aggiunto" >}}) page.

## Site security

The site has perimeter CCTV, access control and fire protection and intruder alarm systems. Connectivity is provided by a dedicated fibre-optic line and industrial Wi-Fi, needed for the WMS to operate in the warehouse areas. The Consortium states that it operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## Contract options

The options differ by area and duration:

- **space in a multi-client warehouse**, for small stocks or short periods, such as seasonal peaks;
- **dedicated area or building**, from 500 to 3,000 square metres, for clients with stable volumes;
- **combined covered and open storage**, for supply chains that handle sensitive goods together with materials that can be kept outdoors.

Rates are calculated per pallet space or per square metre, based on duration, volumes and number of movements; inbound and outbound handling is quoted separately.

## Sectors and territory

Storage serves supply chains with different requirements: from packaged [agri-food]({{< relref "/settori/agroalimentare" >}}) products to [automotive]({{< relref "/settori/automotive" >}}) and [manufacturing]({{< relref "/settori/manifatturiero" >}}) components, as well as stock for [e-commerce]({{< relref "/settori/ecommerce" >}}) and for [large-scale retail]({{< relref "/settori/gdo-retail" >}}). The site is about 8.4 km by road from [Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/latina" >}}), 14.8 km from [Cisterna di Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/cisterna-di-latina" >}}), 33.5 km from [Aprilia]({{< relref "/logistica/aprilia" >}}) and 49.8 km from [Pomezia]({{< relref "/logistica/pomezia" >}}). Distances from the other areas [south of Rome]({{< relref "/logistica/roma-sud" >}}) are given on the [Location]({{< relref "/posizione" >}}) page.

## Requesting a quote

For a storage quote, please state the type of goods, format and weight of the load units, number of pallets or square metres required, expected storage duration, average number of inbound and outbound movements per month, any temperature requirements and additional services such as order picking or [transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}). Requests can be sent via the [contact form]({{< relref "/" >}}#contatti).
