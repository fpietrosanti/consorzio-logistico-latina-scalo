Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo operates a logistics hub on Via della Dinamica, in the Tor Tre Ponti area of Latina Scalo (LT), with 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open areas. The consortium brings together five companies with more than 50 years of combined experience and provides third-party logistics for businesses in the province of Latina and the southern quadrant of Rome.

The covered space is organised in modular warehouses of 500 to 3,000 square metres each, with clear heights of up to 10 metres, set up for pallet racking. The site includes temperature-controlled storage areas, yards for articulated-lorry manoeuvring and covered loading bays. The warehouse runs on a WMS (warehouse management system), and the consortium states that it operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

The services are the same for all sectors: storage, handling with picking, packing, labelling, palletising and cross-docking, road transport with a dedicated fleet, last-mile distribution and document management. What changes from one sector to another are the regulations to be met, the storage conditions and the specifications of end customers.

For each sector, the sector pages describe the local production context, the operations the consortium can carry out and the main regulatory references, from the GDP guidelines for medicinal products to Regulation (EC) No 852/2004 for food and the ADR agreement for dangerous goods. The regulations are given for information only: authorisations, certifications and the specific requirements of each flow are to be verified at the quotation stage.

Latina Scalo can be reached from the SS148 Pontina and the SS7 Appia and lies about 50 km by road from Pomezia and about 70 km from Rome EUR.
