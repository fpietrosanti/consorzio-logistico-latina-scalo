Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo operates a logistics hub on Via della Dinamica, in the Tor Tre Ponti area of Latina Scalo (LT), with 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open areas. The catchment area comprises three areas: the province of Latina, the municipalities of the Metropolitan City of Rome to the south and south-east of the capital, and the southern quadrant of Rome. For each area the site gives road distances and indicative car journey times from the hub, calculated with the OSRM routing engine; journey times are longer for heavy goods vehicles.

## The three areas

### Province of Latina

The [province of Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/provincia-di-latina" >}}) comprises 33 municipalities, from the Aprilia and Cisterna di Latina belt, bordering the Metropolitan City of Rome, to the border with Campania. The main route is the SS148 Pontina, alongside the SS7 Appia and, inland, the SS156 dei Monti Lepini, which links Latina to Frosinone and the A1 motorway network. Latina Scalo is home to Latina railway station, on the Rome–Formia–Naples line. There are four detailed pages:

- [Pianura Pontina]({{< relref "/logistica/pianura-pontina" >}}): Latina, Aprilia, Cisterna di Latina, Pontinia, Sabaudia, Sezze, Sermoneta and the municipalities of the land reclamation area;
- [Monti Lepini and Ausoni]({{< relref "/logistica/lepini-ausoni" >}}): the hill municipalities along the SS156, from Cori to Sonnino;
- [Sud Pontino and Gulf of Gaeta]({{< relref "/logistica/sud-pontino-golfo-di-gaeta" >}}): Terracina, Fondi, Formia, Gaeta, Minturno and the Aurunci hinterland;
- [Pontine Islands]({{< relref "/logistica/isole-ponziane" >}}): Ponza and Ventotene, reached by sea from Formia and Anzio.

### Rome South

The [Rome South]({{< relref "/logistica/roma-sud" >}}) page covers the municipalities of the Metropolitan City of Rome to the south and south-east of the capital. This area includes the Santa Palomba industrial zone, between Rome and Pomezia, the Pomezia–Santa Palomba intermodal terminal and the logistics hubs of the Valle del Sacco along the A1. The sub-areas are:

- [Roman coast]({{< relref "/logistica/litorale-romano" >}}): Pomezia, Ardea, Anzio, Nettuno and Fiumicino;
- [Castelli Romani]({{< relref "/logistica/castelli-romani" >}}): from Velletri to Frascati, along the SS7 Appia;
- [Valle del Sacco and Prenestina area]({{< relref "/logistica/valle-del-sacco-area-prenestina" >}}): Valmontone, Colleferro, Artena, Palestrina and the municipalities near the A1.

### Rome

The [Rome]({{< relref "/logistica/roma" >}}) page and the profile of the [southern quadrant of Rome]({{< relref "/logistica/roma-quadrante-sud" >}}) cover the districts and localities of the Municipality of Rome between the Pontina, the Laurentina, the Appia and the A1: EUR, Torrino, Spinaceto, Castel Romano, Trigoria, Cecchignola, Ostia, Acilia, Magliana, Ponte Galeria, Cinecittà, Anagnina, Romanina and Tor Vergata.

## How the hub serves the areas

The consortium, which brings together five companies with more than 50 years of combined experience, acts as a third-party logistics provider with a single point of contact. [Storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}) takes place in the modular warehouses, set up for pallet racking, and in the open areas. [Handling]({{< relref "/servizi/handling" >}}) includes picking, packing, labelling, palletising and [cross-docking]({{< relref "/servizi/cross-docking" >}}). Road [transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}) with a dedicated fleet covers last-mile distribution in the areas described and B2B deliveries across Italy.

Distances from the hub vary considerably. Central Latina is about 8.4 km by road, Cisterna di Latina about 14.8 km, Velletri about 25.1 km, Aprilia about 33.5 km, Pomezia about 49.8 km and EUR about 70.4 km. The port of Gaeta is about 70.1 km away and Fiumicino about 89.5 km. The individual area pages list all municipalities and localities with their journey times, in a table generated from routing data.

For businesses in south Rome and Rome, the Latina Scalo hub is an area separate from the Pomezia and Santa Palomba logistics district, located about 50 km further south along the Pontina. For businesses in the province of Latina it is a third-party warehouse close to the main Pontine industrial zones: Latina Scalo, Cisterna di Latina, Aprilia, Pontinia and Mazzocchio.

## Requesting a quotation

For a quotation, state the municipality of origin or delivery, type of goods, number of pallets or square metres required, duration of storage, frequency of movements and additional services. Requests can be sent via the [contact form]({{< relref "/" >}}#contatti).
