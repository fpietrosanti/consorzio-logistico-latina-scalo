Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo serves businesses in the southern quadrant of the Municipality of Rome from its hub on Via della Dinamica, in the Tor Tre Ponti area of Latina Scalo (LT), with 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open areas. The area comprises the districts and localities between the SS148 Pontina, Via Laurentina, the SS7 Appia and the A1, from the Ostia coast to Anagnina and Tor Vergata. The full list of localities, with distances, is on the [southern quadrant of Rome]({{< relref "/logistica/roma-quadrante-sud" >}}) page.

## Economic and logistics profile

The southern quadrant of the capital combines residential districts, office functions and production areas along the main radial roads. EUR hosts the offices of large groups and of public administration; along the Pontina lies the Castel Romano industrial zone, at km 23, covering 266 hectares and managed by the Consorzio Industriale del Lazio. Further south-east, straddling the border with Pomezia, the Santa Palomba zone covers 483 hectares and, according to the ZLS Strategic Development Plan, hosts logistics operators such as DHL Supply Chain, GLS, STEF, Rifline and Cab Log and large retail chain platforms run by third parties.

On the eastern side of the quadrant, between Cinecittà, Anagnina, Romanina and Tor Vergata, the network relies on the Grande Raccordo Anulare ring road and the A1 Rome South branch, which links the capital to the A1 Rome–Naples motorway. On the western side, between Magliana, Ponte Galeria and Ostia, the main access is the Rome–Fiumicino motorway, with the Cargo City of Leonardo da Vinci airport, Italy's busiest airport by passengers, and the Rome–Fiumicino logistics platform, whose rail terminal is under study.

## Infrastructure

The link to Latina Scalo is the SS148 Pontina, which starts at EUR and is a dual carriageway with two lanes in each direction as far as Borgo Piave (Latina). For the districts to the east of the capital the alternative is the SS7 Appia, which crosses the Castelli Romani and descends to Velletri and Cisterna di Latina. The Pomezia–Santa Palomba intermodal terminal, on the Rome–Naples line via Formia, ranks first in Lazio by intermodal transport units handled. The Rome–Latina motorway project is under review: the Pontina remains the main route between the two cities.

## Flows to Latina Scalo

According to the routing calculation, the localities closest to the hub are Morena (about 53.3 km by road), Santa Palomba (about 54.8 km), Capannelle (about 56.9 km) and Castel Romano (about 57.1 km). EUR is about 70.4 km away, Magliana about 72.1 km, Ostia about 84.4 km. From localities on the Pontina and the coast the route follows the SS148; from Cinecittà, Anagnina and Capannelle the fastest route descends via the Appia; from Tor Vergata and Romanina the calculation runs via the A1 branch and the provincial road network.

## Business requirements and services

For Rome-based businesses, the Pontine hub is a warehouse outside the city, useful when space in the urban area is insufficient or volumes require dedicated warehouses. Typical uses are:

- [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}) of reserve stock, product archives and seasonal goods, with scheduled replenishment of stores or urban warehouses;
- [cross-docking]({{< relref "/servizi/cross-docking" >}}) and [transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}) for distribution to southern Lazio and central-southern Italy;
- [third-party logistics]({{< relref "/servizi/logistica-3pl" >}}) for [e-commerce]({{< relref "/settori/ecommerce" >}}) and [textile and fashion]({{< relref "/settori/tessile-moda" >}}) businesses, with [document management]({{< relref "/servizi/gestione-documentale" >}}) of transport documents (DDT) and delivery notes;
- storage on [open yards]({{< relref "/servizi/piazzali-scoperti" >}}) for [construction]({{< relref "/settori/edilizia" >}}) companies working on sites in the quadrant.

## Requesting a quotation

For a quotation, state the district or locality of collection and delivery, type of goods, number of pallets or square metres required, duration of storage, frequency of deliveries in the city and additional services. Requests can be sent via the [contact form]({{< relref "/" >}}#contatti).
