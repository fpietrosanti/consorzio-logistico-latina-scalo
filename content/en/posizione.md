## The site

Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo operates in Via della Dinamica, in the Tor Tre Ponti area of Latina Scalo (LT). The site comprises 19,000 square metres of covered space, in modular buildings of 500 to 3,000 square metres, and 25,000 square metres of open space. Latina Scalo lies on the Tyrrhenian corridor between Rome and Naples and serves the Pontine industrial base and the southern districts of Rome.

The Latina Scalo area is home to the industrial area of the same name managed by the Consorzio Industriale del Lazio, which extends over about 448 hectares across the municipalities of Latina, Sermoneta and Sezze; the operational headquarters of the Consorzio Industriale is in the Tor Tre Ponti locality.

## Road network

Latina Scalo can be reached from the SS148 Pontina and the SS7 Appia. The Pontina, managed by ANAS (the Italian state road operator), connects Rome to Terracina over about 109 km. Between Rome and Borgo Piave (Latina) it is a dual carriageway with two lanes in each direction; between Borgo Piave and Terracina it continues with one lane in each direction. On the section between Pomezia and Latina north the speed limit has been lowered from 110 to 80 km/h.

The SS156 Monti Lepini road connects Latina to Frosinone and the A1 motorway network. Alternatively, the A1 junctions at Valmontone and Colleferro can be reached via Cisterna di Latina and Velletri on ordinary roads.

Since October 2026 the Cisterna–Valmontone link road has been under construction; it will connect the Pontina to the A1 near Valmontone. The route, about 31.5–33 km long, includes junctions at Campoverde, Cisterna di Latina, Lariano and Labico; the road will be a toll-free main rural road. There is no confirmed opening date.

The Rome–Latina motorway project is under review and has no opening date: today the Pontina remains the main route between Rome and Latina.

## Rail

Latina Scalo is home to Latina railway station, on the Rome–Formia–Naples line. The station is served by FL7 regional trains and by Intercity services on the Tyrrhenian corridor.

On the site of the former Latina Scalo sugar refinery there is an intermodal terminal, active since 2000, which is currently not operational. The Lazio regional mobility, transport and logistics plan (PRMTL) provides for its return to service and identifies it as a candidate terminal for temperature-controlled, agri-food and pharmaceutical supply chains. There is no record of an active rail siding serving the Consortium's site.

The nearest active intermodal terminal is Pomezia–Santa Palomba, on the Rome–Naples line via Formia: it ranks first in Lazio for intermodal transport units handled, about 60,000 a year according to the PRMTL, with 6 intermodal and 5 conventional tracks. The Santa Palomba area is about 54.8 km by road from the site. Private rail freight terminals connected to the national network operate in Frosinone and Ferentino. The Frosinone Interporto (freight village), by contrast, is reported as abandoned and its operating company is in liquidation.

## Ports

The nearest commercial port is Gaeta, about 70.1 km by road from the site. It is part of the Port System Authority of the Central-Northern Tyrrhenian Sea, together with Civitavecchia and Fiumicino, and handled about 1.8 million tonnes of goods in 2025, mainly bulk.

Civitavecchia is the main port in Lazio, with Ro-Ro and automotive traffic and a growing focus on refrigerated cargo. In 2025 the Civitavecchia, Fiumicino and Gaeta port system handled more than 13 million tonnes of goods. The container and Ro-Ro ports of Campania, Naples and Salerno, can be reached from Latina via the A1 or along the SS7 via Formia.

## Airport

Leonardo da Vinci airport in Fiumicino, Italy's busiest passenger airport, is about 89.5 km by road. The Roma–Fiumicino logistics platform is planned next to Cargo City; its rail terminal is under study.

## Lazio Simplified Logistics Zone

The Lazio Simplified Logistics Zone (ZLS, Zona Logistica Semplificata), established by Prime Ministerial Decree (DPCM) of 17 November 2025, comprises 64 municipalities, 15 of them in the province of Latina, and the ports of Civitavecchia, Fiumicino and Gaeta. The municipality of Latina is only partly included, for about 280 hectares identified on a cadastral basis, that is by sheet and parcel.

In ZLS areas eligible for regional aid, companies can claim a tax credit on investments in capital goods, extended for the years 2026–2028. Eligibility depends on the cadastral parcel of the investment and requires prior notification to the Agenzia delle Entrate (Italian Revenue Agency). The ZLS also provides for simplified administrative procedures and a dedicated one-stop shop (SUAP).

This description covers the regime in general. Inclusion of the Via della Dinamica plot in the ZLS has not been verified: it must be checked by comparing the site's sheet and parcel with the cadastral analysis annexed to DGR 291/2025 (regional council resolution). Lazio is not included in the ZES Unica (single special economic zone).

## Territory served

The site serves companies in the [province of Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/provincia-di-latina" >}}), from Aprilia and Cisterna to Terracina and Fondi in the southern Pontine area, and the [south of Rome]({{< relref "/logistica/roma-sud" >}}), Pomezia and Santa Palomba areas, as well as the metropolitan area of [Rome]({{< relref "/logistica/roma" >}}). The services available at the site are described on the [Logistics services]({{< relref "/servizi" >}}) page.
