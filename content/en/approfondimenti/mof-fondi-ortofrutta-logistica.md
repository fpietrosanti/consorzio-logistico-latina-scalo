The Fondi wholesale food market (Centro Agroalimentare all'Ingrosso di Fondi, MOF ScpA), in the south of the province of Latina, handles «quasi 10 milioni di quintali» (almost 10 million quintals) of goods a year, about 1 million tonnes, according to figures published by its operator. The market covers 335 hectares and hosts 93 concession-holding companies, including 7 agricultural cooperatives grouping about 2,500 growers, with about 3,000 people on site every day. It is classified as an agri-food market of national interest and, together with the Rome agri-food centre (CAR) in Guidonia, is one of the two wholesale markets of national importance in Lazio identified by the Region. For logistics in the [southern Pontine area]({{< relref "/logistica/provincia-di-latina" >}}) it is the main generator of road freight traffic.

## The size of the market

The operator describes a two-tier system. The 93 concession holders operate in the internal area, spread across more than seven halls; the external area has about 50 fruit and vegetable businesses and processing units, as well as a network of small and medium-sized supplier firms. The MOF gives a reference figure of about 2,000 local farms. The Lazio regional mobility, transport and logistics plan (PRMTL), on the other hand, estimates about 130 companies operating in the agri-food centre, «una buona percentuale» (a good percentage) of which offer product processing at the customer's request; the difference between the two figures depends on the scope considered.

According to the PRMTL, the MOF is not dedicated only to trading: it hosts units for the primary processing of fruit and vegetables and stands for large-scale retailers, with the aim of improving quality controls and reducing costs in the face of competition from Mediterranean and Far Eastern countries. Residue testing is carried out by MOF LAB, the in-house testing laboratory.

## Supply catchment and destinations

The operator places the market in a central-southern catchment area (southern Lazio, Campania, Puglia, Sicily) which, according to its figures, accounts for about 60% of Italian fruit and vegetable production. For the local catchment the MOF gives 20,000 cultivated hectares, of which 4,500 under protected cultivation, 935 under organic cultivation and 10,000 under integrated or controlled pest management.

The stated destinations are the major Italian cities, large-scale retail and the Mediterranean basin. The logistics model is typical of wholesale markets: arrivals from growers and from southern markets, consolidation and processing in the market, and onward shipments to retail distribution centres, wholesalers and destination markets.

## Cold storage and platforms

The facilities stated by the operator include a refrigeration plant with a capacity of up to 50,000 quintals and two temperature-controlled logistics platforms totalling 7,000 square metres, with 38 loading bays. These figures point to a transit function rather than long-term storage: refrigerated capacity corresponds to about 0.5% of the stated annual volumes, consistent with goods that enter and leave the market within a short time.

## Lorries, roads and rail

The PRMTL reports operator estimates that the MOF sees a daily flow of more than 300 articulated lorries. Like the CAR in Guidonia, the market has no rail connection. Fondi lies on the SS7 Appia, about 56.3 kilometres by road from the Latina Scalo industrial area according to the OSRM route calculation for cars; connections to Rome run via the Appia and the Pontina, those to Campania via the Appia and Formia.

To reduce the share of road transport, the PRMTL provides, as a medium-term measure, for the design and construction of a rail freight yard in Fondi. The Plan identifies two areas: a pick-up and delivery track about 380 metres long could be built in the first, and the loading and unloading track with service facilities in the second, on about 47,000 square metres. The Plan specifies that modal shift must be assessed through a feasibility study, which will also have to identify a rail operator willing to run the service. The Plan points to the proximity of other traffic-generating nodes: the port of Gaeta, the Formia and Gaeta fish market and the stone industry district.

The same document sets out an alternative scenario: if the MOF yard were not built or did not prove viable, and if the Latina Scalo terminal did not reach critical mass on its own with the chemical-pharmaceutical and agri-food chains, the two shares of demand could be combined at the Latina node alone.

## Public grants for logistics capacity

By resolution No 911 of 9 October 2025, the Lazio regional government approved guidelines and criteria for the grants to the CAR and the MOF provided for by Art. 51 of Regional Law 14/2021, allocating 350,000 euros from the 2025 budget to strengthening the logistics capacity of the two markets. The resolution notes that the MOF and the CAR are both ranked for the NRRP (National Recovery and Resilience Plan) funding for the development of agri-food logistics (Mission 2, Component 1, Investment 2.1, with a national allocation of 800 million euros), and states that the regional grants fund investments complementary to those admitted at national level. Under the European rules referred to in the resolution, the funded infrastructure must be made available to operators on an open and non-discriminatory basis.

## The ZLS and the southern Pontine area

The municipality of [Fondi]({{< relref "/logistica/fondi" >}}) is in the Lazio ZLS (Simplified Logistics Zone) for 69.70 hectares, in an area eligible for regional aid under Article 107(3)(c), according to Annex 2 to DGR 291/2025. In the southern Pontine area, [Formia]({{< relref "/logistica/formia" >}}) (124 hectares), [Gaeta]({{< relref "/logistica/gaeta" >}}) (142.61 hectares plus 17.53 of port areas), Minturno, Itri, Castelforte and Santi Cosma e Damiano are also in the ZLS, all in 107(3)(c) areas; [Terracina]({{< relref "/logistica/terracina" >}}) and Monte San Biagio are in the ZLS but not in a 107(3)(c) area. Companies investing in capital goods on included and eligible parcels can access the 2026-2028 ZLS tax credit, subject to a cadastral check.

## The Latina Scalo site

Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo, in Via della Dinamica, is about 56.3 kilometres by road from Fondi and 38.2 kilometres from Terracina, on the route linking the southern Pontine area to the Latina area and the [southern quadrant of Rome]({{< relref "/logistica/roma-sud" >}}). The site has 19,000 square metres of covered space with temperature-controlled storage areas and 25,000 square metres of yards, and offers [cross-docking and order picking]({{< relref "/servizi/handling" >}}), [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}) and [road transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}) with B2B deliveries. The [location]({{< relref "/posizione" >}}) page gives distances from other places in the province.
