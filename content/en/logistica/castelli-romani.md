Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo serves businesses in the Castelli Romani from its hub on Via della Dinamica, in the Tor Tre Ponti area of Latina Scalo (LT), which comprises 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open areas. The area comprises seventeen municipalities of the Metropolitan City of Rome in the Colli Albani, from Velletri to Frascati and Colonna. Velletri, the nearest municipality, is about 25.1 km by road from the hub; Lariano and Lanuvio about 34 km, Genzano di Roma about 36.8 km, Albano Laziale about 42.8 km, Frascati about 50.3 km and Ciampino about 51.1 km.

## Economic and logistics profile

The Castelli Romani have more than 350,000 inhabitants in total. The most populous municipalities are Velletri (about 52,500), Marino (about 46,300), Albano Laziale (about 39,700) and Ciampino (about 38,600). Wine growing is a long-established activity in the area, with designation-of-origin wines such as Frascati.

The largest production areas lie on the edges of the Colli: to the west towards the Pontina, Aprilia and Santa Palomba; to the south towards Velletri and Cisterna di Latina; to the north towards Ciampino, Via Anagnina and the Grande Raccordo Anulare ring road. Ciampino is home to G. B. Pastine airport, Rome's second airport. The Pomezia and Santa Palomba logistics district and the 368-hectare Cisterna di Latina industrial zone are a short distance from the southern Castelli municipalities.

## Roads and rail

The SS7 Appia crosses the Castelli from Ciampino to Velletri and continues towards Cisterna di Latina and Latina Scalo. The internal road network, with the SP217 dei Laghi and the SR218, connects the municipalities on the Tusculan side. Colonna and Monte Porzio Catone are closer to the A1 and the A1 Rome South branch, via which the routing calculation gives the fastest route to the hub. Lariano will be served by one of the junctions of the Cisterna–Valmontone link road, under construction since October 2026, which will connect the Pontina to the A1 toll-free. By rail, the FL4 regional line links Rome with Frascati, Albano Laziale and Velletri.

## Flows to Latina Scalo

From the municipalities along the Appia, Velletri, Genzano di Roma, Ariccia and Albano Laziale, the route descends directly towards Cisterna and Latina Scalo. From the municipalities on the northern side, Frascati, Grottaferrata, Rocca di Papa and Rocca Priora, journey times increase because of the passage through town centres and hill roads: the values in the table range from about 55 to about 70 minutes by car. For articulated lorries heading to historic centres it is advisable to agree accessible loading points.

## Business requirements and services

For businesses in the Castelli, the Latina Scalo hub is a warehouse below the hill towns, reached via the Appia. The main uses are:

- [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}) of wine and packaged [agri-food]({{< relref "/settori/agroalimentare" >}}) products, including in the temperature-controlled areas;
- [value-added services]({{< relref "/servizi/lavorazioni-valore-aggiunto" >}}) such as labelling, kit assembly and palletising for [large retail chains]({{< relref "/settori/gdo-retail" >}});
- [third-party logistics]({{< relref "/servizi/logistica-3pl" >}}) for [e-commerce]({{< relref "/settori/ecommerce" >}}), with WMS and [document management]({{< relref "/servizi/gestione-documentale" >}});
- [open yards]({{< relref "/servizi/piazzali-scoperti" >}}) for [construction]({{< relref "/settori/edilizia" >}});
- [transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}) with a dedicated fleet for deliveries in the Castelli and towards Rome.
