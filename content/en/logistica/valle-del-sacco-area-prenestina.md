Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo serves businesses in the Valle del Sacco and the Prenestina area from its hub on Via della Dinamica, in the Tor Tre Ponti area of Latina Scalo (LT), which comprises 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open areas. The area comprises fifteen municipalities of the Metropolitan City of Rome between the A1 Rome–Naples motorway, the Monti Prenestini and the Rome side of the Lepini. Artena is about 35.2 km by road from the hub, Segni about 37.9 km, Valmontone about 39.9 km, Colleferro about 42.4 km and Palestrina about 46 km.

## Economic and logistics profile

The Valle del Sacco is one of Lazio's logistics hubs along the A1. Colleferro, home to the aerospace industry (Avio), hosts SLIM, an integrated multimodal logistics system that has been partly built, placed by the ZLS Strategic Development Plan between the Cisterna–Valmontone corridor, the A1 and the Rome–Cassino railway line. Colleferro is in the Lazio ZLS (Simplified Logistics Zone), but not in an Article 107(3)(c) area, and therefore has no access to the ZLS tax credit.

Trade publications cite in the area, a few kilometres from the A1 exit, the Unieuro hub serving nine regions of central and southern Italy, a 30,000-square-metre GXO multi-client centre, a 23,000-square-metre GLP warehouse and an Amazon distribution centre.

The Prenestina area, with Palestrina, Zagarolo, San Cesareo, Gallicano nel Lazio, Cave and Genazzano, is crossed by Via Casilina and Via Prenestina and connected to Rome by the A1 and the A1 Rome South branch. The municipalities of Montelanico, Carpineto Romano and Gorga, on the Rome side of the Lepini, are small towns: Gorga has about 670 inhabitants, Montelanico about 2,000, Carpineto Romano about 4,000.

## Roads and rail

The main route is the A1 Rome–Naples motorway, with the Valmontone and Colleferro toll stations, also connected to Rome by the A1 Rome South branch. The Valmontone and Colleferro toll stations, together with Frosinone, are the reference points for heavy goods vehicles travelling from Latina Scalo to the A1. Until the Cisterna–Valmontone link road opens (under construction since October 2026, with junctions at Campoverde, Cisterna di Latina, Lariano and Labico), the connection with Latina Scalo uses ordinary roads via Artena, Velletri and Cisterna. By rail, the Rome–Cassino line serves the stations of Valmontone and Colleferro-Segni-Paliano; at Ferentino and Frosinone, on the same corridor, there are rail freight terminals connected to the national network.

## Flows to Latina Scalo

Routes to the hub descend from the Valle del Sacco via the provincial network (SP12, SP81a, SP8 and SP3) towards Cisterna di Latina and Latina Scalo. Montelanico, Carpineto Romano and Gorga are connected from the opposite side, via the SS156 dei Monti Lepini. Flows run in two directions: goods travelling north from the province of Latina to the distribution centres on the A1, and stock moving from the Colleferro and Valmontone area down to the Pontine market and the Sud Pontino.

## Business requirements and services

For businesses in the Valle del Sacco, the Latina Scalo hub is a base towards the province of Latina and the coast. The main uses are:

- [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}) of stock in modular warehouses of 500 to 3,000 square metres, for [manufacturing]({{< relref "/settori/manifatturiero" >}}) and [automotive]({{< relref "/settori/automotive" >}});
- [cross-docking]({{< relref "/servizi/cross-docking" >}}) for redistribution to Latina, Terracina, Fondi and the Gulf of Gaeta;
- [open yards]({{< relref "/servizi/piazzali-scoperti" >}}) for [construction]({{< relref "/settori/edilizia" >}}) materials and bulky goods;
- [third-party logistics]({{< relref "/servizi/logistica-3pl" >}}) for [e-commerce]({{< relref "/settori/ecommerce" >}}) and [large retail chains]({{< relref "/settori/gdo-retail" >}}), with [transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}) by a dedicated fleet and national B2B deliveries.

## Requesting a quotation

For a quotation, state the municipality of collection or delivery, type of goods, number of pallets or square metres required, duration of storage, frequency of transfers and additional services. Requests can be sent via the [contact form]({{< relref "/" >}}#contatti).
