## A single point of contact for the logistics chain

Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo provides third-party logistics (3PL) services from the Via della Dinamica site, Tor Tre Ponti area, Latina Scalo (LT). The client entrusts [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}), [handling]({{< relref "/servizi/handling" >}}), [value-added services]({{< relref "/servizi/lavorazioni-valore-aggiunto" >}}), [transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}) and [document management]({{< relref "/servizi/gestione-documentale" >}}) to a single point of contact.

The Consortium brings together five companies with more than 50 years of combined experience. The site comprises 19,000 square metres of covered space, in modular buildings of 500 to 3,000 square metres with clear height up to 10 metres, and 25,000 square metres of open space.

## What is outsourced

When outsourcing logistics, the client transfers to the operator the physical and information activities that follow the production or purchase of the goods:

- **receipt and storage** on racking, on the floor, in temperature-controlled areas or in an [open yard]({{< relref "/servizi/piazzali-scoperti" >}});
- **order preparation**, with picking, packing, labelling and palletising;
- **operations on request**, such as kit assembly and repacking;
- **dispatch**, with the Consortium's dedicated fleet or with carriers designated by the client;
- **documents and data**: transport documents (DDT, Italian delivery notes), traceability of movements and information exchange with the client's management system.

The client retains commercial control, that is orders, price lists and customer relations, while the Consortium carries out operations according to written and agreed procedures.

## The cost model

In the 3PL model the client does not invest in its own warehouse, transport vehicles or management software. Rates are calculated per pallet space or per square metre of storage, per inbound and outbound movement, per order line or per shipment, depending on the combination of services chosen. Logistics costs therefore follow actual volumes, within the minimum conditions set by the contract.

## Stages of the relationship

### Flow analysis

Work starts from the client's historical data: item master data with dimensions, weights and packaging units; average and peak stock; number of orders and order lines; destinations and carriers used. These data determine the space required, the type of storage and the organisation of the picking areas.

### Operating plan

On the basis of the analysis, the Consortium defines the assigned space, in a multi-client warehouse or a dedicated building, receiving and dispatch times, put-away and picking rules and procedures for anomalies, returns and inventories.

### Systems integration

The client's master data, advance shipping notices and orders are transmitted to the Consortium's WMS (warehouse management system); in return the WMS sends back confirmations of receipts, stock levels and completed shipments. Format and frequency of exchanges are agreed at start-up.

### Start-up and ongoing management

Goods are transferred to the site according to an agreed schedule, with stock counted and reconciled on receipt. During ongoing operations the client has a single contact for all activities; volumes, anomalies and service levels are reviewed in periodic meetings.

## Site facilities

Activities rely on covered loading and unloading bays, yards for articulated lorry manoeuvring, vehicle parking areas, industrial shelving and temperature-controlled areas. The site has perimeter CCTV, access control, fire protection and intruder alarm systems, a dedicated fibre-optic connection and industrial Wi-Fi. The Consortium states that it operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## Who it is for

Third-party logistics in Latina Scalo is suited to companies without their own warehouse in southern Lazio, to companies that need to handle seasonal peaks without expanding their own plant and to manufacturers that want to separate logistics from the production area. The supply chains served include [agri-food]({{< relref "/settori/agroalimentare" >}}), [pharmaceuticals]({{< relref "/settori/farmaceutico" >}}), [automotive]({{< relref "/settori/automotive" >}}), [e-commerce]({{< relref "/settori/ecommerce" >}}), [construction]({{< relref "/settori/edilizia" >}}), [textiles and fashion]({{< relref "/settori/tessile-moda" >}}), [large-scale retail]({{< relref "/settori/gdo-retail" >}}) and [manufacturing]({{< relref "/settori/manifatturiero" >}}).

The site is about 8.4 km by road from [Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/latina" >}}), 33.5 km from [Aprilia]({{< relref "/logistica/aprilia" >}}), 49.8 km from [Pomezia]({{< relref "/logistica/pomezia" >}}) and 70.4 km from [Rome EUR]({{< relref "/logistica/eur" >}}). For companies in the southern Pontine area, [Terracina]({{< relref "/logistica/terracina" >}}) is about 38.2 km away and [Fondi]({{< relref "/logistica/fondi" >}}) about 56.3 km. The territory is described on the pages on the [province of Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/provincia-di-latina" >}}), the [south of Rome]({{< relref "/logistica/roma-sud" >}}) and [Rome]({{< relref "/logistica/roma" >}}).

For a third-party logistics quote, please state the type of goods, average and peak stock in pallets or square metres, number of orders and order lines per month, main delivery destinations, additional services required, management system in use and planned start date.
