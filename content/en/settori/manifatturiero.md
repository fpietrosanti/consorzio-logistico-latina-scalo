Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo operates a logistics hub in Latina Scalo (LT), on Via della Dinamica in the Tor Tre Ponti area, with 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open areas, serving manufacturers in the province of Latina and the southern quadrant of Rome that outsource the storage of raw materials, semi-finished and finished goods. The consortium, made up of five companies with more than 50 years of combined experience, acts as a third-party logistics (3PL) provider with a single point of contact.

## Industrial zones in the area

The area between south Rome and Latina includes the industrial zones of Castel Romano, Santa Palomba, Aprilia, Cisterna di Latina, Latina Scalo, Pontinia and Mazzocchio. The Latina Scalo industrial zone covers about 448 hectares across the municipalities of Latina, Sermoneta and Sezze. The Consorzio Industriale del Lazio, formed by the merger of five industrial consortia, has its operational headquarters in Latina, in the Tor Tre Ponti area.

The Aprilia, Cisterna and Latina production cluster specialises in the manufacture of medicinal products and pharmaceutical preparations; together, the provinces of Latina and Frosinone generate more than half of Lazio's exports.

## Simplified Logistics Zone

The Lazio Simplified Logistics Zone (ZLS), established by Prime Ministerial Decree (DPCM) of 17 November 2025, comprises 64 municipalities, 15 of them in the province of Latina. The Municipality of Latina is one of the Pontine municipalities partly included in the ZLS, for about 280 hectares identified on a cadastral basis. The ZLS provides for simplified administrative procedures and a dedicated one-stop shop (SUAP).

In ZLS areas eligible for regional aid, businesses can access a tax credit on investment in capital goods, extended for the years 2026–2028. Eligibility depends on the cadastral parcel of the investment and requires prior notification to the Agenzia delle Entrate (Italian Revenue Agency). Whether the consortium's plot is included in the ZLS has not been verified and is not claimed.

## Connections

Latina Scalo can be reached from the SS148 Pontina and the SS7 Appia; the SS156 dei Monti Lepini links Latina to Frosinone and the A1 motorway network. Since October 2026 the Cisterna–Valmontone link road has been under construction; it will connect the Pontina to the A1 near Valmontone. Road distances are about 15 km to Cisterna di Latina, about 34 km to Aprilia, about 40 km to Valmontone, about 42 km to Colleferro and about 50 km to Pomezia.

## Operations available

For manufacturers the consortium carries out:

- **storage of inbound raw materials and components**, on industrial racking or block-stacked, in modular warehouses of 500 to 3,000 square metres with clear heights of up to 10 metres;
- **intermediate storage** of semi-finished and finished goods between production and distribution;
- **storage in open areas**, 25,000 square metres, for materials and equipment suitable for outdoor storage;
- **handling**: picking, packing, labelling and palletising;
- **cross-docking** for goods in transit;
- **road transport** with a dedicated fleet and B2B deliveries across Italy;
- **document management**: transport documents (DDT), delivery notes, traceability and integration with the client's management systems via the WMS (warehouse management system).

The site has yards for articulated-lorry manoeuvring, covered loading and unloading bays, parking areas, perimeter video surveillance, access control, and fire and intrusion protection systems. The consortium states that it operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, which is relevant for production organised over several shifts.

## Sector regulations and requirements

Storage on behalf of manufacturers involves safety and product rules that vary with the product category:

- **Legislative Decree (D.Lgs.) 81/2008** governs health and safety at work, including the use of lifting equipment and racking; for pallet racking the technical reference is standard **UNI EN 15635**;
- chemical substances and mixtures are subject to **Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 (REACH)** and **Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008 (CLP)**, with obligations on labelling, safety data sheets and segregation of incompatible substances;
- road transport of dangerous goods is governed by the **ADR agreement**;
- storage of combustible materials above certain quantities is subject to the fire prevention checks of **Presidential Decree (D.P.R.) 151/2011**.

The consortium does not state on this page any ADR qualifications or management-system certifications such as ISO 9001. Permitted quantities of dangerous substances, point loads and the requirements of the client's quality systems are to be verified at the quotation stage.

## Requesting a quotation

For a quotation, state the type of goods (raw materials, components, semi-finished goods, finished goods), the weight and dimensions of the unit loads, the number of pallets or square metres required, the share of covered and open storage, the frequency of replenishment to production, whether any classified substances are involved and the management system in use.
