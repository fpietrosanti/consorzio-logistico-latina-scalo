Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo operates a logistics hub in Latina Scalo (LT), on Via della Dinamica in the Tor Tre Ponti area, with 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open areas. The site lies in the production area that includes Aprilia, Cisterna di Latina and Latina, which specialises in the manufacture of medicinal products and pharmaceutical preparations. The consortium, made up of five companies, provides storage, handling, transport and document management for third parties.

## The Pontine pharmaceutical cluster

In 2025 Latina was the second Italian province for pharmaceutical industry exports, according to analysis by the Frosinone-Latina Chamber of Commerce based on ISTAT data. Together, the provinces of Latina and Frosinone generate more than half of Lazio's exports.

The Strategic Development Plan of the Lazio Simplified Logistics Zone (ZLS) identifies the manufacture of medicinal products and pharmaceutical preparations as the specialisation of the Aprilia, Cisterna and Latina area. The ZLS, established by Prime Ministerial Decree (DPCM) of 17 November 2025, comprises 64 municipalities, 15 of them in the province of Latina; these include Aprilia, Cisterna di Latina and Latina, the latter included in part, for about 280 hectares identified on a cadastral basis.

The regional mobility, transport and logistics plan identifies the Latina Scalo rail terminal, currently not in operation, as a candidate terminal for temperature-controlled, agri-food and pharmaceutical supply chains, and provides for its reopening.

## Distances from production sites

Road distances from the site are about 15 km to Cisterna di Latina, about 34 km to Aprilia, about 50 km to Pomezia and about 55 km to Santa Palomba, a district of Pomezia. Fiumicino airport is about 90 km away by road. Latina Scalo can be reached from the SS148 Pontina and the SS7 Appia.

## Operations available

For companies in the sector the consortium provides:

- **storage** on industrial racking, in modular warehouses of 500 to 3,000 square metres with clear heights of up to 10 metres;
- **temperature-controlled storage areas**, with conditions to be defined for each product;
- **handling**: picking, packing, labelling and palletising;
- **road transport** with a dedicated fleet and B2B deliveries across Italy;
- **document management**: transport documents (DDT), delivery notes, movement traceability and integration with the client's management systems via the WMS (warehouse management system).

Site security includes perimeter video surveillance, access control, and fire and intrusion protection systems. Connectivity is based on dedicated fibre optics and industrial Wi-Fi. The consortium states that it operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

The division into modular warehouses allows the flows of different clients to be physically separated, a frequent requirement in the sector's specifications.

## Sector regulations and requirements

Pharmaceutical logistics is among the most heavily regulated. The main references are:

- **Legislative Decree (D.Lgs.) 219/2006**, which makes the wholesale distribution of medicinal products for human use subject to an authorisation issued by the Region and provides for a responsible person;
- the **Guidelines of 5 November 2013 on good distribution practice (GDP)** of medicinal products for human use (2013/C 343/01), which govern premises, supplier qualification, temperature mapping and monitoring, management of returns and recalls, and transport;
- **Regulation (EU) 2017/745** on medical devices and **Regulation (EU) 2017/746** on in vitro diagnostic medical devices, which require distributors to ensure storage and transport conditions that comply with those set by the manufacturer;
- for active substances, solvents and reagents classified as dangerous, **Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008 (CLP)** applies to classification and labelling and the **ADR agreement** to road transport.

The consortium does not state on this page any authorisation for the wholesale distribution of medicinal products, GDP compliance or ADR qualifications. The range of products that can be handled, temperature requirements, batch and expiry management and quarantine procedures are to be verified at the quotation stage, based on the client's documentation.

## Requesting a quotation

For a quotation, state the product category (medicinal products, medical devices, packaging materials, other), the authorisations required for the flow, temperature conditions, the number of pallets or square metres needed, how batches and expiry dates are to be managed, shipment frequency and destinations.
