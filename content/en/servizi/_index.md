Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo, formed by five companies with more than 50 years of combined experience, operates a logistics site in Via della Dinamica, Tor Tre Ponti area, Latina Scalo (LT), with 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open space. The buildings are modular, from 500 to 3,000 square metres each, with clear height up to 10 metres and prepared for pallet racking.

At the same site the Consortium carries out, on a third-party basis, goods receipt, racked, floor and yard storage, order picking, value-added services, cross-docking and dispatch. Road transport is carried out with a dedicated fleet, both for last-mile distribution and for B2B deliveries across Italy. Stock levels and movements are recorded in the Consortium's WMS (warehouse management system); transport documents and traceability are handled by the same office that coordinates the warehouse.

The client can contract a single service, for example storage of seasonal stock, or the entire chain with a single point of contact, under the third-party logistics (3PL) model. The Consortium states that it operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

The site is about 8.4 km by road from Latina centre, 14.8 km from Cisterna di Latina, 33.5 km from Aprilia and 49.8 km from Pomezia.
