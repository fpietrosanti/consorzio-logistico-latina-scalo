Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo operates a logistics hub in Latina Scalo (LT), on Via della Dinamica in the Tor Tre Ponti area, with 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open areas, which can be used for the storage and distribution of automotive spare parts, components and accessories. The consortium brings together five companies with more than 50 years of combined experience and manages storage, handling and transport through a single point of contact.

## Infrastructure context

Latina Scalo lies on the Tyrrhenian corridor between Rome and Naples and serves the Pontine production area. The site can be reached from the SS148 Pontina and the SS7 Appia; the SS156 dei Monti Lepini links Latina to Frosinone and the A1 motorway network.

Since October 2026 the Cisterna–Valmontone link road has been under construction; it will connect the Pontina to the A1 near Valmontone. It will be a toll-free main non-urban road, with junctions at Campoverde, Cisterna di Latina, Lariano and Labico. Valmontone and Colleferro, municipalities on the A1, are about 40 km and about 42 km from the site by road.

For sea freight, Civitavecchia is the main port in Lazio, with Ro-Ro and automotive traffic. In 2025 the port system of Civitavecchia, Fiumicino and Gaeta handled more than 13 million tonnes of goods.

## Operations available

For the automotive sector the consortium carries out:

- **storage** of spare parts and components on industrial racking, in modular warehouses of 500 to 3,000 square metres with clear heights of up to 10 metres, set up for pallet racking;
- **storage in open areas** for bulky materials suitable for outdoor storage, on 25,000 square metres;
- **handling**: picking of individual items, packing, labelling and palletising;
- **cross-docking** for goods in transit, with covered loading and unloading bays;
- **road transport** with a dedicated fleet, B2B deliveries across Italy and last-mile distribution to parts dealers, workshops and dealerships;
- **document management**: transport documents (DDT), delivery notes, movement traceability and integration with the client's management systems.

The WMS (warehouse management system) records locations and movements of each item, which is relevant for spare-parts catalogues with many slow-moving lines. The site has dedicated fibre-optic connectivity, industrial Wi-Fi, perimeter video surveillance, access control, and fire and intrusion protection systems. The consortium states that it operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

The yards allow articulated lorries to manoeuvre and vehicles to wait for loading.

For parts distributors that outsource their entire warehouse, the consortium acts as a third-party logistics (3PL) provider, with a single point of contact for storage, handling, transport and documents. The client keeps control of sales and order management through integration between its own management system and the warehouse WMS.

## Sector regulations and requirements

The logistics of spare parts and components involves several sets of rules, depending on the product category:

- **lithium batteries**, loose or installed in equipment, are classified as dangerous goods under the **ADR agreement** for road transport; **Regulation (EU) 2023/1542** on batteries also governs labelling and end-of-life management;
- oils, lubricants, coolants and chemical products, where classified, fall under **Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008 (CLP)**, with obligations on labelling and on the availability of safety data sheets;
- storage facilities exceeding certain quantities of combustible materials are subject to the fire prevention checks laid down by **Presidential Decree (D.P.R.) 151/2011**;
- pallet racking must be used and inspected in accordance with standard **UNI EN 15635**, within the framework of the obligations of **Legislative Decree (D.Lgs.) 81/2008** on health and safety at work.

The consortium does not state any ADR qualifications or management-system certifications on this page. Conditions for storing dangerous goods, permitted quantities and transport arrangements are to be verified at the quotation stage.

## Requesting a quotation

For a quotation, state the type of goods (spare parts, components, tyres, accessories), the number of active items, the number of pallets or square metres required, whether dangerous goods are involved, the average number of order lines per day, destinations and delivery windows.
