Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo serves the municipalities of the Monti Lepini and Ausoni in the province of Latina from its hub on Via della Dinamica, in the Tor Tre Ponti area of Latina Scalo (LT), with 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open areas. The area comprises nine hill municipalities, from Rocca Massima and Cori in the north to Sonnino and Roccasecca dei Volsci in the south-east. Distances from the hub are short: Bassiano is about 16.4 km by road, Cori about 21.8 km, Priverno about 24.4 km, Maenza about 26.9 km and Sonnino about 38.7 km.

## Economic and logistics profile

The Lepini and Ausoni municipalities are small: Priverno, the largest town, has about 13,700 inhabitants, Cori about 10,400, Sonnino about 7,400; six of the nine municipalities have fewer than 5,000 inhabitants. Production activities are spread between the hilltop historic centres and the valley floors, where the main roads run.

The reference industrial site is the Mazzocchio zone of the Consorzio Industriale del Lazio: 445 hectares, 259 of them industrial, in the Municipality of Pontinia, on the border with Priverno and Sonnino. Further north, the municipalities of Cori and Rocca Massima look towards Cisterna di Latina, home to the consortium's 368-hectare zone and the junction of the future Cisterna–Valmontone link road.

Hill agri-food production includes olive growing. According to the ARSIAL profile, Olio Colline Pontine PDO owes its characteristics to the territory and in particular to the Itrana cultivar; the production area includes municipalities of the Lepini and Ausoni.

## Roads and rail

The main route is the SS156 dei Monti Lepini, which runs from Latina to Priverno and up the Amaseno valley to Frosinone and the A1 motorway network. Provincial roads to Maenza, Roccagorga, Prossedi and Roccasecca dei Volsci branch off the SS156; Sonnino is also connected by the SS699. The municipalities on the northern slopes, Cori, Rocca Massima and Bassiano, are served by the provincial network that descends towards Cisterna, Sermoneta and Latina Scalo, including the SP12 and SP35A.

On the Rome–Formia–Naples railway line, the area is served by Priverno-Fossanova station. For freight, the road network remains the reference for now: the Latina Scalo intermodal terminal is not in operation, and the regional mobility, transport and logistics plan provides for its reopening.

## Flows to Latina Scalo

For the municipalities along the SS156, the route to Latina Scalo follows the state road down to the plain. From the northern slopes, the provincial routes are more winding and have significant gradients: for articulated lorries it is advisable to agree accessible loading points, while light vehicles are used for deliveries in the historic centres. The proximity of Latina Scalo makes it possible to concentrate storage in the valley and to supply hilltop businesses with scheduled deliveries.

## Business requirements and services

For businesses with limited space on their own premises, the hub offers [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}) in modular warehouses of 500 to 3,000 square metres, suitable for finished-goods stock, packaging materials and seasonal goods. For [agri-food]({{< relref "/settori/agroalimentare" >}}) there are temperature-controlled areas and [value-added services]({{< relref "/servizi/lavorazioni-valore-aggiunto" >}}) such as labelling and palletising; for [construction]({{< relref "/settori/edilizia" >}}) and [manufacturing]({{< relref "/settori/manifatturiero" >}}) the [open yards]({{< relref "/servizi/piazzali-scoperti" >}}). [Transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}) with a dedicated fleet handles local distribution and B2B deliveries in Italy, with [document management]({{< relref "/servizi/gestione-documentale" >}}) of transport documents (DDT) and delivery notes.

## Requesting a quotation

For a quotation, state the municipality and address of collection or delivery, any access restrictions for heavy goods vehicles, type of goods, number of pallets or square metres, duration of storage and additional services. Requests can be sent via the [contact form]({{< relref "/" >}}#contatti).
