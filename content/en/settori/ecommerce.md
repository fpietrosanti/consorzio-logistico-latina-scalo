Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo operates a logistics hub in Latina Scalo (LT), on Via della Dinamica in the Tor Tre Ponti area, with 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open areas, including order fulfilment services for companies that sell online. The site is about 70 km by road from Rome EUR and about 50 km from Pomezia. The consortium, made up of five companies, manages storage, order preparation and distribution through a single point of contact.

## Distances to delivery areas

The province of Latina and the southern quadrant of Rome are the two catchment areas closest to the site. Distances, calculated by road for a car in normal traffic conditions, are as follows:

| Destination | Road distance | Indicative car journey time |
|---|---|---|
| Central Latina | about 8 km | about 13 minutes |
| Velletri | about 25 km | — |
| Aprilia | about 34 km | about 36 minutes |
| Pomezia | about 50 km | about 50 minutes |
| Rome EUR | about 70 km | about 70 minutes |
| Fiumicino | about 90 km | about 86 minutes |

Journey times are longer for vans and heavy goods vehicles. The main link to Rome is the SS148 Pontina, which between Rome and Latina (Borgo Piave) is a dual carriageway with two lanes in each direction. The Rome–Latina motorway project is under review: the Pontina remains the main route between Rome and Latina.

## Operations available

For e-commerce clients the consortium carries out:

- **goods receipt and storage** on industrial racking, in modular warehouses of 500 to 3,000 square metres with clear heights of up to 10 metres;
- **picking** by order, both for shipments to individual customers and for replenishment of resellers;
- **packing and labelling** of outbound parcels;
- **palletising** for B2B shipments and reseller replenishment;
- **road transport** with a dedicated fleet, last-mile distribution and B2B deliveries across Italy;
- **document management**: transport documents (DDT), delivery notes, shipment tracking and integration with the client's management systems.

The warehouse runs on a WMS (warehouse management system), with dedicated fibre-optic connectivity and industrial Wi-Fi. The site has perimeter video surveillance, access control, and fire and intrusion protection systems. The consortium states that it operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week: order cut-off times and courier collections are agreed with the client.

The modular warehouses, of 500 to 3,000 square metres each, allow areas of different sizes to be allocated according to volumes. Availability of additional space in peak periods, such as end-of-year promotional campaigns, is to be agreed at the quotation stage.

## Sector regulations and requirements

Distance selling involves obligations that fall on the seller but affect how logistics is organised:

- **Legislative Decree (D.Lgs.) 206/2005 (Consumer Code)** gives consumers the right to withdraw within 14 days of delivery, with direct effects on return flows and refund times;
- **Regulation (EU) 2023/988** on general product safety (GPSR) includes fulfilment service providers among economic operators, with obligations to cooperate with market surveillance authorities;
- food products sold online are subject to hygiene and consumer information rules, in particular **Regulation (EC) No 852/2004** and **Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011**;
- products such as lithium batteries, aerosols and perfumes may be classed as dangerous goods under the **ADR agreement**.

The consortium does not state on this page any integrations with specific e-commerce platforms or any ADR qualifications. Integrations, returns procedures and the handling of dangerous goods are to be verified at the quotation stage.

## Requesting a quotation

For a quotation, state the product category, the number of items (SKUs), the average number of orders per day and seasonal peaks, average parcel dimensions, the sales channel (own website, marketplace, B2B), the management system or platform in use, the main destinations and expected delivery times.
