Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo serves businesses in the Sud Pontino and the Gulf of Gaeta from its hub on Via della Dinamica, in the Tor Tre Ponti area of Latina Scalo (LT), which comprises 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open areas. The area runs from Terracina to the border with Campania and comprises thirteen municipalities. Terracina is about 38.2 km by road from the hub, Fondi about 56.3 km, Gaeta about 70.1 km, Formia about 73.8 km.

## Economic and logistics profile

The Sud Pontino has three production reference points. The first is the Centro Agroalimentare all'Ingrosso di Fondi (MOF), an agri-food market of national interest. According to its operator, the MOF covers 335 hectares, hosts 93 concession-holding companies, handles almost 10 million quintals (about 1 million tonnes) of goods a year and has a central cold store and two temperature-controlled platforms. The market is not served by rail; the regional mobility, transport and logistics plan provides for a dedicated rail terminal. Around the MOF and along the coastal belt between Terracina and Fondi there is a large greenhouse vegetable sector.

The second reference point is the port of Gaeta, part of the Port System Authority of the Central-Northern Tyrrhenian Sea together with Civitavecchia and Fiumicino. In 2025 it handled 1,786,317 tonnes, almost exclusively liquid and dry bulk; in the first half of 2026 traffic rose by 13%. The port is a Comprehensive node of the TEN-T network. Formia is the passenger and freight port for the islands of [Ponza and Ventotene]({{< relref "/logistica/isole-ponziane" >}}).

The third reference point is the industrial areas of the former Consorzio Sud Pontino, now part of the Consorzio Industriale del Lazio: Penitro, Ponzanello–Mamurrano and Castagneto in Formia, Penitro and Parchi in Minturno, Monte Conca in Gaeta, Pantaniello in Santi Cosma e Damiano and the Fondi industrial area. The Consorzio Industriale del Lazio owns the Formia–Gaeta railway line, which is being reactivated.

Nine municipalities in the area are in the Lazio ZLS, established by Prime Ministerial Decree (DPCM) of 17 November 2025: these include Formia (124 hectares), Gaeta (142.61 hectares plus 17.53 hectares of port areas) and Fondi (69.70 hectares).

## Roads and rail

The SS7 Appia is the area's main route: from Terracina it runs through Monte San Biagio, Fondi and Itri and descends to Formia and Minturno. The SR213 Flacca links Terracina, Sperlonga, Gaeta and Formia along the coast. The SS148 Pontina, with one lane in each direction between Borgo Piave and Terracina, connects the area to Latina. For Minturno, Santi Cosma e Damiano, Spigno Saturnia and Castelforte, the routing calculation gives the route via the A1 and SS156, through Frosinone and Cassino, as the fastest; journey times, between 103 and 118 minutes, are among the longest in the province. The Rome–Formia–Naples line serves the stations of Monte San Biagio-Terracina Mare, Fondi-Sperlonga, Itri, Formia-Gaeta and Minturno-Scauri.

## Flows to Latina Scalo

Two flows meet in the area: fruit, vegetables and packaged products travelling north from the Fondi district to distribution centres in northern Lazio and Rome, and goods arriving from Rome and northern Italy to be stored and redistributed in the Gulf. Latina Scalo lies on the route between the two catchment areas, about 56 km from Fondi along the Appia and about 50 km from Pomezia along the Pontina.

## Business requirements and services

For [agri-food]({{< relref "/settori/agroalimentare" >}}) businesses the hub offers temperature-controlled areas, [cross-docking]({{< relref "/servizi/cross-docking" >}}) for consolidating loads to [large retail chains]({{< relref "/settori/gdo-retail" >}}) and [value-added services]({{< relref "/servizi/lavorazioni-valore-aggiunto" >}}) such as labelling and palletising. Businesses working with the port of Gaeta or in the area's [manufacturing]({{< relref "/settori/manifatturiero" >}}) sector can use the [open yards]({{< relref "/servizi/piazzali-scoperti" >}}) and [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}) in warehouses of 500 to 3,000 square metres. [Transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}) with a dedicated fleet connects Latina Scalo to the Gulf municipalities.

## Requesting a quotation

For a quotation, state the municipality of collection or delivery, type of goods, temperature requirements, number of pallets or square metres, duration of storage and the season calendar. Requests can be sent via the [contact form]({{< relref "/" >}}#contatti).
