Key Notes on Corrections:
- Ħżin vs. Ħażna: "Ħżin" means "ugly", while "ħażna" means "storage/warehousing". This is a critical meaning correction.
- Ħaxix vs. Ħxejjex: "Ħaxix" means grass or marijuana, whereas "ħxejjex" is the correct plural for vegetables in an agricultural context.
- f' vs. fi: The preposition "f'" is only used before vowels and the letter 'h'. Before consonants (like 'L' in Latina or 'V' in Via), "fi" must be used.
- Palits vs. Pallets: The correct logistics loanword in Maltese is "pallets" (or "paletts"), making the derived verbs "ippallettizzata" and "ippallettizzati".
- mmaniġġ- vs. immaniġġ-: The correct Maltese orthography for Form II passive participles and verbal nouns uses the "im-" prefix (e.g., immaniġġjat, maniġġar).

- Interlokutur: A direct calque from Italian. "Punt ta' kuntatt" (point of contact) is the natural and standard B2B Maltese equivalent.
