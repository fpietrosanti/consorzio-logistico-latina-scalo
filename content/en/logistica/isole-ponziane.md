Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo provides businesses and suppliers on Ponza and Ventotene with mainland storage and transport to the embarkation ports from its hub on Via della Dinamica, in the Tor Tre Ponti area of Latina Scalo (LT). The hub comprises 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of open areas. Of the two main embarkation ports for the islands, Anzio is about 34.4 km by road and Formia about 73.8 km.

## Economic and logistics profile

The Pontine Islands (Isole Ponziane) belong to the province of Latina and form two municipalities: Ponza, with about 3,300 inhabitants, and Ventotene, with about 700. The economy depends on summer tourism, fishing and services; the population present rises sharply in the summer months, and with it demand for food, beverages, consumables and supplies for accommodation and catering businesses.

All goods arrive by sea. Public service connections are operated by Laziomar, which has held the concession since 2014 and now operates under a bridging contract extended to 17 December 2026. In July 2026 the Lazio Region launched the tender for maritime continuity services with Ponza and Ventotene: five years, a maximum value of about 18.8 million euros a year and a total budget of about 118 million. The concession covers passengers, vehicles and freight.

## Ports and lines

According to the tender notice, all ferry lines depart from Formia: Formia–Ponza, Formia–Ventotene and Formia–Ventotene–Ponza. The ro-pax ferries must have at least 500 seats and 60 lane metres of garage space; the ro-ro ferry at least 120 lane metres for commercial vehicles and cars. Fast craft serve the Anzio–Ponza, Formia–Ponza, Ponza–Ventotene and Formia–Ventotene lines. For freight carried on commercial vehicles, the reference port is therefore Formia; Anzio is connected to Ponza by fast passenger craft. The Terracina–Ponza line is not among the lines put out to tender.

## Flows to Latina Scalo

From Latina Scalo, Formia is reached via the SS7 Appia, through Terracina, Fondi and Itri; Anzio via the provincial network towards the coast. The typical chain involves collection from mainland suppliers, storage in Latina Scalo, consolidation into loads compatible with sailing times and transfer to the embarkation port. The times in the table are calculated by car and do not include the crossing or waiting times at embarkation, which depend on the operator's timetable and sea conditions.

## Business requirements and services

For island businesses, warehouse space on the island is limited: keeping stock on the mainland allows goods to be received in several deliveries, depending on space available on sailings and on how the season develops. The hub offers:

- [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}) of seasonal stock for accommodation, catering and retail businesses, including in the temperature-controlled areas for [agri-food]({{< relref "/settori/agroalimentare" >}}) products;
- [cross-docking]({{< relref "/servizi/cross-docking" >}}) to combine into a single load the supplies of several suppliers destined for the same customer;
- [value-added services]({{< relref "/servizi/lavorazioni-valore-aggiunto" >}}) such as palletising and labelling by recipient;
- [transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}) with a dedicated fleet to the embarkation port, with [document management]({{< relref "/servizi/gestione-documentale" >}}) of transport documents (DDT) and delivery notes;
- [open yards]({{< relref "/servizi/piazzali-scoperti" >}}) for [construction]({{< relref "/settori/edilizia" >}}) materials awaiting embarkation.

## Requesting a quotation

For a quotation, state the island and final recipient, intended embarkation port, type of goods, temperature requirements, number of pallets, shipment frequency and period of the season. Requests can be sent via the [contact form]({{< relref "/" >}}#contatti).
