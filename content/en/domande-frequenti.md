  # ZLS regulations
  - group: "ZLS regulations"
    q: "What is the Lazio Simplified Logistics Zone?"
    a: "The Lazio Simplified Logistics Zone (ZLS), established by Prime Ministerial Decree (DPCM) of 17 November 2025, comprises 64 municipalities, 15 of them in the province of Latina, and the ports of Civitavecchia, Fiumicino and Gaeta. It provides for simplified administrative procedures, a dedicated one-stop shop (SUAP) and a tax credit for eligible investments."
  - group: "ZLS regulations"
    q: "Is the Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo site in the ZLS?"
    a: "The municipality of Latina is only partly included in the Lazio ZLS, for about 280 hectares identified by cadastral sheet and parcel. Inclusion of the Via della Dinamica plot, where Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo is based, has not been verified and requires comparison with the cadastral analysis of DGR 291/2025."
  - group: "ZLS regulations"
    q: "Who can obtain the ZLS tax credit in the province of Latina?"
    a: "The ZLS tax credit is available to companies investing in capital goods on ZLS parcels eligible for regional aid, for the years 2026–2028. It requires prior notification to the Agenzia delle Entrate (Italian Revenue Agency). Pontinia, Sermoneta, Sezze and Terracina are in the ZLS but outside the eligible areas."
  - group: "ZLS regulations"
    q: "Is Latina part of the ZES Unica?"
    a: "No. The ZES Unica (single special economic zone) covers Abruzzo, Basilicata, Calabria, Campania, Molise, Apulia, Sicily, Sardinia and, since November 2025, Marche and Umbria. Lazio, and therefore the province of Latina, is not included. For Lazio the applicable regime is the Simplified Logistics Zone (ZLS)."
