The insights section collects analysis of the logistics system of the province of Latina and the southern quadrant of Rome, together with practical guides for companies that outsource storage, handling and transport. Each article lists the sources of the data it cites: legislation, documents of the Lazio Region and of the ministries, ISTAT (Italian National Institute of Statistics) figures, data from the Frosinone-Latina Chamber of Commerce and the trade press.

The topics follow three strands: the infrastructure and regulations that are changing how goods move through the area (the Simplified Logistics Zone, the Cisterna–Valmontone link road, rail terminals, the ports of Gaeta and Civitavecchia); the production chains that generate the main freight flows (the pharmaceutical industry, fruit and vegetables and Kiwi Latina PGI, the Fondi wholesale food market); and the operational and contractual tools of third-party logistics (calculating the cost of a pallet space, service level indicators, transport documents, cross-docking, distribution to Rome). The glossary collects definitions of the terms most widely used in the sector.
