In early October 2026 work began, from the Campoverde site on the SS148 Pontina state road, on the Cisterna-Valmontone link road, the connection of about 33 kilometres that will join the Pontina to the A1 motorway near Valmontone. The project, presented in Latina by the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport (MIT) on 4 March 2026, has a cost given by the MIT as 1.3 billion euros. For freight flows in the [province of Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/provincia-di-latina" >}}), which today depend almost entirely on the Pontina towards Rome and on ordinary roads towards the A1 toll gates, it is the first large-scale road construction site after decades of plans.

## The link road: route, costs and works

According to the MIT press release, the route is about 33 kilometres long and includes 18 viaducts and 4 tunnels; the presentation was attended by the minister **Matteo Salvini**, the President of the Lazio Region **Francesco Rocca** and the government's extraordinary commissioner **Antonio Mallamo**. Giornale del Lazio, which reported the start of works on 25 October 2026, gives a design length of 31.5 kilometres and a cost of 1.1 billion euros; the sources do not explain the difference from the Ministry's figures.

Four junctions are planned: Campoverde, Cisterna di Latina, Lariano and Labico. Originally designed as a motorway, the project was downgraded to a main non-urban road and will therefore be toll-free. Again according to Giornale del Lazio, the works will be split into lots of about 50-60 million euros each, to be started in parallel; of the roughly 1,100 land parcels to be expropriated, 950 have been acquired, with three appeals pending. Commissioner Mallamo attributed the small number of disputes to the public consultation process and to the updating of prices.

The sources give no opening date. The Strategic Development Plan of the Lazio ZLS (Simplified Logistics Zone) indicated completion in 2028; the October 2026 article instead speaks of completion estimated at ten years, with the possibility of opening in sections. The detailed design is expected to be completed by the end of 2026.

## The Pontina today

The SS148 Pontina runs for about 109 kilometres from Rome to Terracina and has been managed by ANAS, the national road agency, since 2019, after a period under regional management. Between Rome and Borgo Piave, on the outskirts of Latina, it has two separate carriageways with two lanes in each direction; from Borgo Piave to Terracina the cross-section narrows to one lane in each direction. According to the road's data sheet based on ANAS figures, the speed limit on the section between Pomezia and Latina north has been lowered from 110 to 80 km/h.

On 29 April 2021 ANAS presented a 137 million euro upgrade plan for the road in two phases: the first focused on safety work and maintenance at critical points, the second on work along the entire length. In later press releases the company announced, among other measures, the replacement of the central reservation barrier and resurfacing on several sections.

The Lazio regional mobility, transport and logistics plan (PRMTL) describes the Pontina as a road with «cronici problemi di traffico» (chronic traffic problems) and infrastructure shortcomings, and identifies the Pontina and the Ardeatina as the only road accesses to the Pomezia-Santa Palomba intermodal terminal, the largest in Lazio by intermodal transport units handled.

## The Rome-Latina motorway

The Rome-Latina motorway project has been revised: according to Giornale del Lazio the Aprilia bypass has been dropped, the route will follow that of the existing Pontina and the entry into Rome has yet to be designed. On 16 May 2026 the MIT announced the appointment of the tender committee for the construction of the motorway; the announcement gives no amounts, lengths or timescales. The October 2026 article reports that the motorway will not be completed within the current regional council term. At present there is therefore no opening date, and the Pontina remains the main route between Rome and Latina.

## Expected effects on freight flows

The link road mainly changes the connection between the Agro Pontino and the A1. Today, heavy goods vehicles heading to Naples or to the north without passing through Rome reach the Valmontone and Colleferro toll gates via Cisterna and Velletri on ordinary roads, or the Frosinone toll gate via the SS156 Monti Lepini road, the link indicated by the PRMTL for the Latina Scalo industrial area. According to Giornale del Lazio, with the new road the journey time from Campoverde to the motorway at Valmontone would fall to 25-30 minutes, compared with over an hour today: this is an estimate reported by the press, to be verified once the road is open.

Planning documents already identify three effects:

- **an alternative to the Pontina for traffic not bound for Rome.** Shipments heading for the A1 will be able to avoid the stretch of the Pontina between Aprilia and the Grande Raccordo Anulare (GRA, Rome's orbital motorway) and the crossing of the Rome node;
- **access to the industrial areas.** The PRMTL provides for upgrading the local roads around the Latina Scalo terminal (Strada della Chiesuola, Viale delle Industrie, Via Conca, Strada Podgora) «fintantoché non verrà realizzata» (until the link road to the A1 is built);
- **integration with the Valle del Sacco.** The ZLS Plan places the Colleferro production areas between the Cisterna-Valmontone cross route and the A1, close to the Rome-Cassino railway line, and points to the Integrated Multimodal Logistics System (SLIM) there, which has been partly built.

The first beneficiary is the industrial area of [Cisterna di Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/cisterna-di-latina" >}}), 368 hectares according to the Consorzio Industriale del Lazio, located at the junction with the link road; the ZLS Plan lists there companies in the pharmaceutical and packaging chains such as Nuova Ompi (Stevanato Group), BSP Pharmaceuticals and Plasta Rei, together with those in Latina. Further south, the municipalities of [Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/latina" >}}), [Sermoneta]({{< relref "/logistica/sermoneta" >}}) and Sezze would gain more direct access to the A1 via Cisterna.

For operators, the construction phase will carry its own costs: lots opened in parallel, junctions under construction and works on the Pontina at Campoverde may affect delivery times over the coming years. Opening in sections, if confirmed, will make the benefits gradual.

## The Latina Scalo site

The warehouse of Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo, in Via della Dinamica, is about 14.8 kilometres by road from Cisterna di Latina, 39.9 kilometres from Valmontone and 42.4 kilometres from Colleferro (OSRM route calculation for cars; journey times for heavy goods vehicles are longer). The site, with 19,000 square metres of covered space and 25,000 square metres of yards for manoeuvring articulated lorries, can be reached from the SS148 Pontina and the SS7 Appia and serves destinations in the [province of Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/provincia-di-latina" >}}) and the [southern quadrant of Rome]({{< relref "/logistica/roma-sud" >}}) with [road transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}). Up-to-date distances are on the [location]({{< relref "/posizione" >}}) page and in the pages for [Valmontone]({{< relref "/logistica/valmontone" >}}) and [Colleferro]({{< relref "/logistica/colleferro" >}}).
