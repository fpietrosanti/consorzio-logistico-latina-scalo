Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo operates a logistics hub in Latina Scalo (LT), on Via della Dinamica in the Tor Tre Ponti area, with 25,000 square metres of open areas and 19,000 square metres of covered space, which can be used for the storage and distribution of building materials and products. The consortium brings together five companies with more than 50 years of combined experience and manages storage, handling and transport for third parties.

## Area served

Latina Scalo lies on the Tyrrhenian corridor between Rome and Naples and serves the Pontine production area. The area between south Rome and Latina includes the industrial zones of Castel Romano, Santa Palomba, Aprilia, Cisterna di Latina, Latina Scalo, Pontinia and Mazzocchio. The Latina Scalo industrial zone covers about 448 hectares across the municipalities of Latina, Sermoneta and Sezze.

The site can be reached from the SS148 Pontina and the SS7 Appia; the SS156 dei Monti Lepini links Latina to Frosinone and the A1 motorway network. Road distances are about 8 km to central Latina, about 15 km to Cisterna di Latina, about 25 km to Velletri, about 34 km to Aprilia, about 50 km to Pomezia and about 57 km to Castel Romano.

Since October 2026 the Cisterna–Valmontone link road has been under construction; it will connect the Pontina to the A1 near Valmontone. It will be a toll-free main non-urban road, with junctions at Campoverde, Cisterna di Latina, Lariano and Labico.

## Operations available

For building-materials distributors, manufacturers and construction companies the consortium carries out:

- **storage in open areas**, 25,000 square metres, for materials suitable for outdoor storage;
- **covered storage** on industrial racking or block-stacked, in modular warehouses of 500 to 3,000 square metres with clear heights of up to 10 metres, for bagged binders, insulation, waterproofing systems and finishes;
- **handling**: picking, packing, labelling and palletising, including assembling the materials for a single job;
- **cross-docking** for loads in transit between manufacturer and builders' merchant;
- **road transport** with a dedicated fleet and B2B deliveries across Italy;
- **document management**: transport documents (DDT), delivery notes, movement traceability and integration with the client's management systems.

The yards allow articulated lorries to manoeuvre and vehicles to park; the loading and unloading bays are covered. The site has perimeter video surveillance, access control, and fire and intrusion protection systems, which is relevant for materials stored outdoors. The consortium states that it operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

The WMS (warehouse management system) records warehouse stock; the criteria for allocating stock to individual jobs or sites, including materials in the open areas, are defined with the client.

## Sector regulations and requirements

Construction products and some site materials are subject to rules that also concern storage and transport:

- **Regulation (EU) No 305/2011** on construction products requires CE marking and a declaration of performance; distributors must ensure that storage and transport conditions do not jeopardise product compliance;
- paints, solvents, resins, adhesives and some additives are classified under **Regulation (EC) No 1272/2008 (CLP)** and, for transport, may fall within the **ADR agreement**;
- storage of combustible materials above certain quantities, such as insulation, timber and plastics, is subject to the fire prevention checks of **Presidential Decree (D.P.R.) 151/2011**;
- stacking of heavy unit loads and the use of racking fall within the obligations of **Legislative Decree (D.Lgs.) 81/2008** and standard **UNI EN 15635**.

The consortium does not state any ADR qualifications or the floor load capacity on this page. Vehicles with crane or tail lift, permitted quantities of flammable materials and point loads are to be verified at the quotation stage.

## Requesting a quotation

For a quotation, state the product families, the weight and dimensions of the unit loads, the share of material to be stored outdoors and under cover, the square metres or number of pallets required, the duration of storage, whether any products are classified as dangerous, the destinations (merchants or building sites) and the unloading method required.
