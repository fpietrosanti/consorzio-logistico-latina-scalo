## 25,000 square metres of open space

Consorzio Logistico Latina Scalo provides 25,000 square metres of open space at the Via della Dinamica site, Tor Tre Ponti area, Latina Scalo (LT). The areas are intended for storing goods that can be kept outdoors: building materials, empty containers, vehicles and equipment, bulky goods. They are in addition to the 19,000 square metres of covered buildings described on the [storage]({{< relref "/servizi/stoccaggio" >}}) page.

Having covered and open space at the same site makes it possible to manage, under a single contract, goods that require an enclosed environment and goods that do not. The [construction]({{< relref "/settori/edilizia" >}}) and [manufacturing]({{< relref "/settori/manifatturiero" >}}) supply chains can thus keep at the same site both stock that needs protection and stock that can be stored outdoors.

## What is stored outdoors

### Building materials

Bricks, blocks, concrete products, pipes, sections and palletised materials with packaging suitable for outdoor use. Yard storage serves building materials distributors and companies supplying construction sites in southern Lazio, in combination with [transport]({{< relref "/servizi/trasporto" >}}) for deliveries to site.

### Empty containers

Empty containers awaiting repositioning or a new load. The nearest commercial port is Gaeta, about 70.1 km by road from the site; the [Santa Palomba]({{< relref "/logistica/santa-palomba" >}}) area, which hosts the Pomezia–Santa Palomba intermodal terminal, is about 54.8 km by road.

### Vehicles and equipment

Commercial vehicles, plant machinery, trailers and site equipment parked between assignments. The yards allow articulated lorries to manoeuvre and the site has vehicle parking areas.

### Bulky goods

Industrial packaging, large components, reels and out-of-gauge packages that do not fit on racking and that, given the nature of their packaging, can be kept outdoors. The same types of goods can be floor-stored in the buildings when weather protection is needed.

## The operating process

### Receipt and recording

On arrival, the vehicle enters the site through access control. The operator checks the goods against the transport document (DDT, Italian delivery note), checks the condition of the units and notes any reservations. Each unit, including containers identified by their number, is recorded in the WMS (warehouse management system) with its storage area.

### Yard allocation

The yard is divided into marked areas, assigned by client and by type of goods. Access lanes are kept clear for heavy vehicle manoeuvring and for the lifting equipment needed for handling. Stack heights and stackability are agreed with the client on the basis of the packaging specifications.

### Inventory

Units in the yard are subject to the same checks as warehouse stock: periodic counts, comparison with WMS records and reporting of discrepancies to the client.

### Dispatch

On the client's order, units are prepared and loaded onto the designated vehicle, with the transport document issued. Dispatches can be combined with [cross-docking]({{< relref "/servizi/cross-docking" >}}) of incoming goods or with deliveries by the Consortium's fleet.

## Covered and open storage under one contract

Many supplies combine units that need protection and units that can be kept outdoors: for example pallets of bagged product, to be kept under cover, and pipes or concrete products, which can be stored in the yard. At the Latina Scalo site the two parts of the stock are recorded in the same WMS and can leave on the same vehicle, with a single transport document and a single contact for the client.

## Security

The site has perimeter CCTV, access control and fire protection and intruder alarm systems, which also cover the outdoor areas. The Consortium states that it operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## Location and territory

Latina Scalo can be reached from the SS148 Pontina and the SS7 Appia; the SS156 Monti Lepini road connects Latina to Frosinone and the A1. From the site, [Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/latina" >}}) centre is about 8.4 km by road, [Cisterna di Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/cisterna-di-latina" >}}) 14.8 km, [Aprilia]({{< relref "/logistica/aprilia" >}}) 33.5 km and [Colleferro]({{< relref "/logistica/colleferro" >}}) 42.4 km. The service is relevant to companies in the [province of Latina]({{< relref "/logistica/provincia-di-latina" >}}) and the southern districts of Rome; the logistics areas [south of Rome]({{< relref "/logistica/roma-sud" >}}) are described on the dedicated page.

## Requesting a quote

For a yard storage quote, please state the type of goods, dimensions and weights of the units, estimated area in square metres or number of containers, expected duration, frequency of inbound and outbound movements, handling equipment required and any need for cover or protection.
